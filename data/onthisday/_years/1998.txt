{{Year in Nepal|1998}}

Events from the year '''1998 in [[Nepal]]'''.

== Incumbents ==

* [[King of Nepal|Monarch]]: [[Birendra of Nepal|Birendra]]
* [[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]]: [[Surya Bahadur Thapa]], and [[Girija Prasad Koirala]]
* [[Chief Justice]]: [[Om Bhakta Shrestha]], and [[Mohan Prasad Sharma]]

== Events ==

* 1 January – Visit Nepal Year 1998 begins.<ref>{{Cite book|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=OsrsAAAAMAAJ|title=Facts about Nepalese Economy|date=1998|publisher=Nepal Society for Applied Economics|language=en}}</ref>
* April – First case of [[Kali River goonch attacks]] is reported.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Five arrested for killing 125 kg 'near threatened' catfish in Almora|url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/dehradun/five-arrested-for-killing-125-kg-near-threatened-catfish-in-almora/articleshow/59772321.cms|url-status=live|access-date=12 July 2021|website=The Times of India|language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210713000800/https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/dehradun/five-arrested-for-killing-125-kg-near-threatened-catfish-in-almora/articleshow/59772321.cms |archive-date=13 July 2021 }}</ref>
* 29 June – Nepalese politician [[Mirza Dilshad Beg]] is assassinated.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Any one who aligns with Dawood Ibrahim is my enemy: Chhota Rajan|url=https://www.indiatoday.in/magazine/interview/story/19980720-any-one-who-aligns-with-dawood-ibrahim-is-my-enemy-chhota-rajan-826730-1998-07-20|url-status=live|access-date=12 July 2021|website=India Today|language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190820093802/https://www.indiatoday.in/magazine/interview/story/19980720-any-one-who-aligns-with-dawood-ibrahim-is-my-enemy-chhota-rajan-826730-1998-07-20 |archive-date=20 August 2019 }}</ref>
* 3–13 October – [[1998 ACC Trophy]] is held in Nepal.<ref>{{Cite web|title=History of Nepali cricket: 5 cricketers you should know|url=https://english.onlinekhabar.com/history-of-nepali-cricket-5-cricketers-you-should-know.html|access-date=12 July 2021|website=OnlineKhabar English News|language=en-GB}}</ref>
* 6–20 December – Nepal won four total medals – one silver and three bronzes at the [[Nepal at the 1998 Asian Games|1998 Asian Games]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Over all medal standings – Bangkok 1998|url=http://www.ocasia.org/Game/MWinner.aspx?AMWCode=17&GCode=1|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100616235841/http://ocasia.org/Game/MWinner.aspx?AMWCode=17&GCode=1|archive-date=16 June 2010|access-date=5 June 2011|work=[[Olympic Council of Asia]]|publisher=ocasia.org}}</ref>
* 31 December – Visit Nepal Year 1998 ends.
