{{Year in Nepal|2003}}

Events from the year '''2003 in Nepal'''.

==Incumbents==
*[[King of Nepal|Monarch]]: [[Gyanendra of Nepal|Gyanendra]]
*[[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]]: [[Lokendra Bahadur Chand]] (until 3 June), [[Surya Bahadur Thapa]] (starting 3 June)
*[[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]]: [[Kedar Nath Upadhyaya]]

== Events ==
===January===
* January 10 - [[Maoism|Maoists]] send a letter threatening journalist [[Ruprekha|Rabin Prasad Thapalia]] with death for an article deemed sympathetic to government forces.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Attacks on the Press in 2003 - Nepal |url=https://www.refworld.org/reference/annualreport/cpj/2004/en/56219 |access-date=2025-05-08 |website=Refworld |language=en}}</ref>
* January 26 - [[Armed Police Force (Nepal)|Armed Police Force]] Inspector General of Police [[Krishna Mohan Shrestha]] is killed along with his wife and bodyguard by Maoists.<ref name="nyt">{{cite web|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2003/01/26/world/nepal-s-police-chief-killed.html|title=Nepal's Police Chief Killed|date=26 January 2003|work=Agence France-Presse|via=The New York Times}}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite web |title=Nepal: A brief history of the conflict |url=https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2003/12/26/nepal-a-brief-history-of-the-conflict |access-date=2025-05-08 |website=Al Jazeera |language=en}}</ref><ref name=":0" />
* January 29 - The Maoists and the government announce a cease fire.<ref name=":1" /><ref name=":2">{{Cite web |title=Amnesty International Report 2004 - Nepal |url=https://www.refworld.org/reference/annualreport/amnesty/2004/en/31847 |access-date=2025-05-08 |website=Refworld |language=en}}</ref>

=== February ===

* February 15 - Journalist Deepak Thapa is reportedly confined to his village by local Maoist leaders in [[Achham District|Accham]] district due to his reporting.<ref name=":0" />

=== April ===

* The first round of peace talks are held between the Nepali government and the Maoists.<ref name=":2" />

=== May ===

* The second round of peace talks are held between the Nepali government and the Maoists.<ref name=":2" />
* May 30 - [[Lokendra Bahadur Chand|Prime Minister Lokendra Bahadur Chand]] resigns following political pressure, and [[Surya Bahadur Thapa]] is appointed Prime Minister.<ref name=":2" />
* May 31 - 18 [[Tibet Autonomous Region|Tibetan]] asylum-seekers are forcibly returned to [[China]] despite international appeals.<ref name=":2" />

===August===
* The third round of peace talks are held between the Nepali government and the Maoists.<ref name=":2" />
* August 17 - Royal Nepal Army executes 19 Maoists in [[Doramba]], [[Ramechhap District|Ramechhap district]].<ref name=":2" />
* August 27 - Maoists unilaterally withdraw from the cease fire.<ref name=":0" /><ref name=":2" /><ref>{{Cite web |title=Nepal: Political Developments and U.S. Relations |url=https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R44303.html |access-date=2025-05-08 |website=www.everycrsreport.com |language=en}}</ref>
* August 28 - Journalist Ramahari Chaulugain is kidnapped near his office in [[Kathmandu]] by unidentified assailants.<ref name=":0" />

===September===
* September 1 - Reli Maya Muktan is reportedly killed by Maoists in Doramba.<ref name=":2" />
* September 7 - [[Nepalese Civil War]]: Journalist [[Gyanendra Khadka]] is killed by Maoist insurgents.<ref name="cpj">{{Cite web|url=http://cpj.org/killed/2003/gyanendra-khadka.php|title = Gyanendra Khadka}}</ref><ref name=":0" />
* September 9 - Editor Subhashankar Kandel is abducted from his home in Kathmandu by security forces, held in custody, and allegedly beaten.<ref name=":0" />
* September 11 - Om Bahadur Thapa is arrested and allegedly tortured in the [[Singha Durbar]] army barracks.<ref name=":2" />
* September 13 -
** [[Miss Nepal 2003|Miss Nepal]] beauty contest is held.  The winner is Priti Sitoula of [[Kathmandu]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://missnepal.com.np/pressrelease.php |title=Miss Nepal - Press |website=missnepal.com.np |access-date=15 January 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060209144626/http://missnepal.com.np/pressrelease.php |archive-date=9 February 2006 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
** [[Jana Aastha National Weekly|Sitaram Baral]] is abducted in Kathmandu by security forces, tortured, and hospitalized after release five days later. Premnath Joshi is also arrested at night from his home, with no charges filed.<ref name=":0" />
*September 20 - Deepak Thapa is arrested and tortured in [[Hanuman Dhoka]] police custody.
*September 21 - Journalist Nawaraj Pahadi is arrested in [[Lamjung District|Lamjung]] for reporting on corruption at a [[Hydroelectricity|hydro power plant]], and detained without charge.<ref name=":0" />
*September 27 - Two girls, 14 and 16 years old, are raped by police officers in [[Banke District|Banke]] district.<ref name=":2" />

=== October ===

* October 13 - Royal Nepal Army fire indiscriminately at a Maoist-organized cultural program at Sharada Higher Secondary School in [[Doti]], killing 4 students.<ref name=":2" />

=== November ===

* November 18 - FNJ official Dhana Bahadur Magar is detained by security forces.<ref name=":0" />

==Births==
*[[October 16]] &ndash; [[Princess Kritika of Nepal]]

==Deaths==
* January 25 &ndash; [[Krishna Mohan Shrestha]]
