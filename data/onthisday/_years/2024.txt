== Events ==
===January===
* 5 January – Nepal stops issuing permits to its citizens to work in [[Russia]] and [[Ukraine]] after 10 Nepalis are killed while serving in the [[Russian Army]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nepal bars citizens from going to Russia or Ukraine for work |url=https://abcnews.go.com/International/wireStory/nepal-bars-citizens-russia-ukraine-work-recruited-fighters-106124536 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240105105003/https://abcnews.go.com/International/wireStory/nepal-bars-citizens-russia-ukraine-work-recruited-fighters-106124536 |url-status=dead |archive-date=5 January 2024 |access-date=2024-01-05 |website=ABC News |language=en}}</ref>

===March===
*4 March – Prime Minister Pushpa Kamal Dahal ends his coalition agreement with the [[Nepali Congress]] and forms a new coalition government with the [[Communist Party of Nepal (Unified Marxist-Leninist)]] and other smaller parties.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nepal's communist parties join forces to form a new coalition government |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-communist-parties-new-government-coalition-ee620c7596f3356de2103b1ad3f86a3c |access-date=2024-03-04 |website=Associated Press |date=4 March 2024 |language=English}}</ref>

===May===
*3 May – The [[Supreme Court of Nepal]] orders the government to limit the number of permits it issues to those seeking to climb [[Mount Everest]] and other mountain peaks in the country to preserve the mountains and their environment.<ref>{{Cite web |title='We are pressuring the mountain too much': Nepal court limits Everest climbing permits |url=https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20240503-nepal-court-orders-limit-on-everest-climbing-permits |access-date=4 May 2024 |website=France 24 |date=4 May 2024 |language=English}}</ref>
*21 May – [[Kailash Sirohiya]], the owner of Nepal's largest news organisation [[Kantipur Publications]], is arrested at company offices in [[Kathmandu]] for alleged violations of  citizenship laws after his citizenship card number is found to share that of another.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Owner of Nepal's largest media organization arrested over citizenship card issue |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-media-owner-arrested-government-citizenship-card-df216010a7aab38ef7eab9bb341a365c |access-date=22 May 2024 |website=Associated Press |date=22 May 2024 |language=English}}</ref>

===June===
*26 June – Two days of heavy rains in Nepal kills 20 people.<ref>{{Cite web |date=June 26, 2024 |title=Heavy rains in Nepal kill 20 in two days amid landslides, lightning strikes |website=[[Reuters]] |url=https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/heavy-rains-nepal-kill-20-two-days-amid-landslides-lightning-strikes-2024-06-26/ }}</ref>
*29 June – Landslides in Nepal kill nine people.<ref>{{Cite web |date=June 29, 2024 |title=Nepal landslides kill nine, including 3 children |website=[[Reuters]] |url=https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/nepal-landslides-kill-nine-including-3-children-2024-06-29/ }}</ref>

===July===
*1 July – A court in [[Sarlahi]] sentences Buddhist religious figure [[Ram Bahadur Bomjon]] to ten years' imprisonment and damage payments of $3,700 for sexually assaulting a minor.<ref>{{Cite web |date=July 1, 2024 |title=Nepalese spiritual leader 'Buddha Boy' sentenced to 10 years in prison for sexual assault on minor |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-spiritual-leader-buddha-boy-jailed-sexual-assault-77736e9c3a29f0e3f8862b9531e3a47f |work=Associated Press}}</ref>
*3 July – The Communist Party of Nepal (Unified Marxist-Leninist) withdraws from Prime Minister Pushpa Kamal Dahal's governing coalition after entering into a coalition agreement with the Nepali Congress.<ref>{{Cite web |date=July 4, 2024 |title=Key partner withdraws support from Nepal's government to join new coalition |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-politics-government-crisis-80a8567e1a9e79f96b4a2225160f80d5 |work=Associated Press}}</ref>
*7 July – At least 11 people are killed and eight others are missing due to heavy rainfall causing [[flash flood]]s and landslides across Nepal.<ref>{{Cite web |date=July 7, 2024 |title=Heavy rains trigger landslides in Nepal, 11 killed, 8 missing |website=[[Reuters]] |url=https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/heavy-rains-trigger-landslides-nepal-11-killed-8-missing-2024-07-07/ }}</ref>
*12 July:
** [[Madan Ashrit Highway disaster]]: At least 11 people are killed and around 51 others are reported missing after two buses are swept off the [[Narayangarh, Chitwan|Narayangarh]] - [[Mugling]] highway in [[Chitwan District]] by a landslide and fall into the [[Trishuli River]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=July 13, 2024 |title=Nepal recovers first body from buses swept away by landslide |website=[[France 24]] |url=https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20240713-nepal-recovers-first-body-from-buses-swept-away-by-landslide }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=July 15, 2024 |title=Rescuers in Nepal recover 11 bodies after a landslide swept 2 buses full of people into a river |website=[[Associated Press]] |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-landslide-buses-river-search-recover-bodies-abd9abbc55fad3a2ca5dda21553df475 }}</ref> About 500 rescuers are involved in the search for survivors.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.reuters.com/business/environment/chances-finding-survivors-slim-after-nepal-landslide-official-says-2024-07-13/ | title=Chances of finding survivors slim after Nepal landslide, official says | work=Reuters | date=13 July 2024 }}</ref>
** Prime Minister Pushpa Kamal Dahal steps down after losing a no-confidence motion filed against him in the [[House of Representatives (Nepal)|House of Representatives]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=July 12, 2024 |title=Nepal's prime minister loses a confidence vote forcing him to step down |website=[[Associated Press]] |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-prime-minister-confidence-vote-parliament-politics-27923b7dbc25d20c6b90a9b8fbf3c8cf}}</ref>
*14 July – [[Khadga Prasad Oli]], the leader of the Communist Party of Nepal (Unified Marxist-Leninist), is named Prime Minister as part of a coalition with the Nepali Congress.<ref>{{Cite web |date=July 14, 2024 |title=The leader of Nepal's largest communist party has been named the country's new prime minister |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-politics-prime-minister-oli-febf3d135e785ebd2d98f374fd86e85c |work=Associated Press}}</ref> He is formally inaugurated on 15 July.<ref>{{Cite web |date=July 15, 2024 |title=Nepal's new prime minister has taken the oath of office at a ceremony in Kathmandu |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-politics-new-prime-minister-oli-bb44de6376ee099e5282f1040fdeebce |work=Associated Press}}</ref>
*24 July – [[2024 Saurya Airlines Bombardier CRJ200 crash]]: Eighteen of 19 people aboard a passenger aircraft are killed during its takeoff at [[Tribhuvan International Airport]] in [[Kathmandu]]. The only survivor is the pilot.<ref>{{Cite web |date=July 24, 2024 |title=Pilot only survivor of Nepal plane crash |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c978m8p6lm6o |work=BBC}}</ref>

===August===
*7 August – A [[Eurocopter AS350]] belonging to [[Air Dynasty]] flying from Kathmandu to [[Syaprubeshi]] crashes into a forested mountain in [[Suryachaur]], [[Nuwakot District]], killing all four Chinese passengers on board and the pilot.<ref>{{Cite web |date=August 7, 2024 |title=5 people killed in a helicopter crash in the mountains northwest of Nepal's capital |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-helicopter-crash-59ec1032369a35b07e4e2fe767b0687e |work=Associated Press}}</ref>
*16 August – The village of [[Thame, Nepal|Thame]] in [[Solukhumbu District]] is inundated by a [[2024 Thame flood|glacial lake outburst flood]] that destroys at least 15 structures.<ref>{{Cite web |date=August 17, 2024 |title=Himalayan village in Nepal hit by icy floods |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvg3pz2py7vo |work=BBC}}</ref>
*22 August – The government lifts a ban on [[TikTok]] that had been imposed since November 2023.<ref>{{Cite web |date=August 23, 2024 |title=Nepal lifts its ban on TikTok imposed for disrupting social harmony |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-tiktok-ban-lifted-social-media-0993fc5402cf17fe2ecf0e70f64699fa |work=Associated Press}}</ref>
*23 August – A bus carrying Indian pilgrims on its way to Kathmandu to [[Pokhara]] plunges from the [[Prithvi Highway]] into the [[Marshyangdi River]] near [[Anbu Khaireni]], killing at least 27 people and injuring 16 others.<ref>{{Cite web |date=August 23, 2024 |title=27 killed after a bus with Indian pilgrims drives off a Nepal highway and crash |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-bus-accident-indian-pilgrims-marsyangdi-f183559f47ed82447bdc899ea44c2ce6 |access-date=August 23, 2024 |website=[[Associated Press]] |language=en}}</ref>
*30 August – [[Palesha Goverdhan]] becomes the first Nepalese athlete to win a medal at a [[Paralympics]] after winning bronze at the women's K44-57&nbsp;kg para taekwondo category in the [[2024 Summer Paralympics]] in [[Paris]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=September 4, 2024 |title=Nepal's first Paralympic medallist returns home to hero's welcome |url=https://www.aljazeera.com/gallery/2024/9/4/nepals-first-paralympic-medallist-returns-home-to-heros-welcome |access-date=September 4, 2024 |website=[[Al Jazeera]] |language=en}}</ref>

===September===
*27 September–1 October – At least 224 people are killed and 24 others are reported missing following [[2024 Nepal floods|nationwide flooding]] and landslides caused by heavy rains.<ref>{{Cite web |date=October 1, 2024 |title=Rescuers search for missing people in Nepal following flooding and landslides that killed 224 |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-flood-landslide-deaths-3d8622e5e258920b013976ce37e155e1 |work=Associated Press}}</ref>

===October===
*8 October – Five Russian climbers are found dead on [[Dhaulagiri]] after being reported missing on 6 October.<ref>{{Cite web |date=October 8, 2024 |title=Five Russians Killed Climbing Mountain in Nepal – Tourism Official |url=https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2024/10/08/five-russians-killed-climbing-mountain-in-nepal-tourism-official-a86609 |work=The Moscow Times}}</ref>
* 9 October – Activist Deepti Gurung is awarded the [[List of Nansen Refugee Award recipients|Nansen Refugee Award]] by the [[United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees]], citing her work in helping amend the country's citizenship laws after her daughters were designated as stateless persons.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/10/9/brazilian-nun-awarded-un-refugee-prize |title=Brazilian nun awarded UN refugee prize |date=9 October 2024 |access-date=9 October 2024 |website=Al Jazeera |language=en}}</ref>
* 18 October – Former deputy prime minister [[Rabi Lamichhane]] is arrested on suspicion of fraud and organised crime involving the alleged embezzlement of funds from a financial cooperative.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20241020-nepal-protesters-clash-with-police-over-politician-s-fraud-charges |title=Nepal protesters clash with police over politician's fraud charges |date=20 October 2024 |access-date=20 October 2024 |website=France 24|language=en}}</ref>

===November===
*15 November – A van falls off a mountain road near [[Shaileshikar]], killing eight people and injuring four others.<ref>{{Cite web |date=15 November 2024 |title=Van falls off mountain highway in Nepal, killing 8 and injuring 4 |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-passenger-van-crash-2921bdf81b26bcb920a7f077e9d1e6fb |work=Associated Press}}</ref>
