The following lists events that happened during '''[[2012]] in [[Nepal]]'''.

==Events==
===May===
* May 5 - At least 13 people are killed and dozens of others are declared missing as a result of a Nepal flash flood after a mountain river burst its banks.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-17967761 | title=Nepal floods: Thirteen dead near Annapurna | date=5 May 2012 | accessdate=14 January 2015 | archive-date=20 August 2014 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140820000229/http://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-17967761 | url-status=live }}</ref>
* May 14 - An [[Agni Air]] flight carrying 21 people [[Agni Air Flight CHT|crashes]] while attempting a landing at the mountainous [[Jomsom Airport]]. Indian child actress [[Taruni Sachdev]] is among the 15 people killed.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.thenews.com.pk/article-48939-Plane-carrying-21-crashes-in-Nepal |title=Plane carrying 21 crashes in Nepal |date=14 May 2012 |accessdate=14 January 2015 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150115230530/http://www.thenews.com.pk/article-48939-Plane-carrying-21-crashes-in-Nepal |archivedate=15 January 2015 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-india-18068761 | title=India Bachchan co-star Taruni dies in Nepal plane crash | date=15 May 2012 | accessdate=14 January 2015 | archive-date=1 May 2020 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200501133308/https://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-india-18068761 | url-status=live }}</ref>
* May 16 - British musician Oz Bayldon, representing Music4children, establishes a [[Guinness World Records|Guinness World Record]] by performing the highest concert on land, at 6,676&nbsp;m (21,246&nbsp;ft), on [[Mera Peak]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/highest-altitude-concert-on-land |title=Highest altitude concert on land |date= |website=[[Guinness World Records]] |access-date=May 26, 2017 |archive-date=May 15, 2017 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170515014615/http://guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/highest-altitude-concert-on-land |url-status=live }}</ref>
* May 28
**Nepal has neither constitution nor a sitting legislature after an attempt to develop a new national constitution was unsuccessful.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.upiasia.com/Top-News/2012/05/28/Nepal-left-without-constitution-Assembly/UPI-26481338215910/ | title=Nepal left without constitution, Assembly | date=28 May 2012 | accessdate=14 January 2015 | archive-date=18 October 2012 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121018181504/http://www.upiasia.com/Top-News/2012/05/28/Nepal-left-without-constitution-Assembly/UPI-26481338215910/ | url-status=live }}</ref>
**The first [[Racemandu]] motorcycle racing event is organized.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://thehimalayantimes.com/business/yamaha-racemandu/|accessdate=February 14, 2018|publisher=The Himalayan Times|title=Yamaha 'Racemandu'|date=8 May 2017|archive-date=14 February 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180214074808/https://thehimalayantimes.com/business/yamaha-racemandu/|url-status=live}}</ref>
* May 31 - Supreme Court Justice Rana Bahadur Bam is assassinated in Lalitpur.<ref>{{cite web |title=Justice Rana Bahadur Bam’s murderers get life in prison |url=https://thehimalayantimes.com/kathmandu/justice-rana-bahadur-bams-murderers-get-life-in-prison/ |website=The Himalayan Times |publisher=APCA Nepal Pvt. Ltd. |accessdate=25 May 2019}}</ref>

===September===
* September 6 - The [[United States]] removes the [[Unified Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist)]] from their terror list, citing "a credible commitment to pursuing peace and reconciliation". In June, a group around party vice-president Mohan Baidya announced a split in the party.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://2009-2017.state.gov/r/pa/prs/ps/2012/09/197411.htm | title=Delisting of the Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist) | date=6 September 2012 | accessdate=14 January 2015 | df=dmy-all | archive-date=30 January 2017 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170130214939/https://2009-2017.state.gov/r/pa/prs/ps/2012/09/197411.htm | url-status=live }}</ref>
