Events from the year '''2002 in Nepal'''.

==Incumbents==
*[[King of Nepal|Monarch]]: [[Gyanendra of Nepal|Gyanendra]]<ref>{{cite web |title=Gyanendra {{!}} king of Nepal |url=https://www.britannica.com/biography/Gyanendra |website=Encyclopedia Britannica |accessdate=2 January 2020 |language=en}}</ref>
*[[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]]: [[Sher Bahadur Deuba]] (until 4 October), [[Lokendra Bahadur Chand]] (starting 11 October)
*[[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]]: [[Keshav Prasad Upadhyaya]] (until 5 December), [[Kedar Nath Upadhyaya]] (starting 5 December)

== Events ==
* January 18 - US Secretary of State [[Colin Powell]] arrives in Nepal supporting the government efforts against fighting Maoist terrorist.<ref>{{cite web |title=Remarks With Prime Minister of Nepal Sher Bahadur Deuba |url=https://2001-2009.state.gov/secretary/former/powell/remarks/2002/7345.htm |publisher=US Department of State |accessdate=13 May 2019|date=2002-01-18 }}</ref>
* February 15 - Members of the breakaway faction of CPN-UML rejoins the party, while some under the leadership of [[CP Mainali]] forms [[Communist Party of Nepal (Marxist–Leninist) (2002)|Communist Party of Nepal (Marxist–Leninist)]] declining to rejoin.
* February 27 - Maoists launch simultaneous attacks in Mangalsen and Sanfebagar of Accham District killing 141 including the Chief District Officer.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://sajha.com/archives/openthread.cfm?threadid=3538|title=the Achham incident could have been averted|last=|first=|date=|website=Sajha.com, Kathmandu Post|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200724134632/http://sajha.com/archives/openthread.cfm?threadid=3538 |archive-date=2020-07-24 |accessdate=11 May 2019}}</ref>
* May 22 - King Gyanendra dissolves parliament on recommendation of Prime Minister Sher Bahadur Deuba and calls for election on November 13.
* May 27
** State of emergency is extended for three months through royal ordinance.
** Maoists attack a Royal Nepal Army base in Khara, Rukum. A dozen soldiers are killed whereas over 100 maoists are killed in the attack.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/south_asia/2044809.stm |website=BBC |accessdate=11 May 2019|title=Nepal troops 'thwart' rebel attack |date=2002-06-14 }}</ref>
* October 3 - Deuba proposes to delay election for a year.
* October 4 - King Gyanendra dismisses Prime Minister Sher Bahadur Deuba terming him incapable.
* November 14 - Maoists attack Khalanga, the district headquarter of [[Jumla District|Jumla]].
