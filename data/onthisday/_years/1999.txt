{{Year in Nepal|1999}}

Events from the year '''1999 in [[Nepal]]'''.

== Incumbents ==

* [[King of Nepal|Monarch]]: [[Birendra of Nepal|Birendra]]
* [[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]]: [[Girija Prasad Koirala]] (1998 – May 1999) and [[Krishna Prasad Bhattarai]]
* [[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]]: [[Mohan Prasad Sharma]] (1998–1999) and [[Keshav Prasad Upadhyaya]] (from December 1999)

== Events ==

* May –  [[1999 Nepalese general election|General elections were held]] from 3 to 17 May.<ref>{{Cite web|date=21 October 2008|title=Previous Election Facts and Figures|url=http://www.election.gov.np/EN/prevelection.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081021001831/http://www.election.gov.np/EN/prevelection.html|archive-date=21 October 2008|access-date=4 January 2021}}</ref>
* 5 September – [[Necon Air Flight 128]] crashes in the west of [[Kathmandu]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Jeziorski|first=Andrzej|date=15 September 1999|title=Nepal suffers second fatal crash|url=https://www.flightglobal.com/nepal-suffers-second-fatal-crash/28489.article|url-status=live|access-date=8 July 2021|website=FlightGlobal|language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200606161117/https://www.flightglobal.com/nepal-suffers-second-fatal-crash/28489.article |archive-date=6 June 2020 }}</ref>
* September and October − [[1999 South Asian Games|8th South Asian Games]] were held in Kathmandu from 25 September to 4 October.<ref>{{Cite news|title=Hosts Nepal set new medals record at South Asian Games|language=en|work=The Kathmandu Post|url=https://kathmandupost.com/sports/2019/12/06/hosts-nepal-set-new-medals-record-at-south-asian-games|access-date=8 July 2021}}</ref>
* November – [[1999 ANFA League Cup|ANFA League Cup]] is held from 20 to 30 November.
* December – [[Indian Airlines Flight 814]] is hijacked in Indian [[airspace]] between [[Kathmandu]], [[Nepal]], and [[Delhi]], India.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Bowing to Terrorism|url=https://frontline.thehindu.com/cover-story/article30253131.ece|url-status=live|access-date=8 July 2021|website=Frontline|language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210708185910/https://frontline.thehindu.com/cover-story/article30253131.ece |archive-date=8 July 2021 }}</ref>
