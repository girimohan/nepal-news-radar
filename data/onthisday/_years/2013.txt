==Incumbents==
*[[President of Nepal|President]]: [[Ram Baran Yadav]] 
*[[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]]: [[Baburam Bhattarai]] (until 14 March), [[Khil Raj Regmi]] (acting) (starting 14 March)
*[[Vice President of Nepal|Vice President]]: [[Parmanand Jha]]
*[[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]]: [[Khil Raj Regmi]]

==Incumbents==
* 14 March - [[Khil Raj Regmi]] became acting Prime minister.

==Events==
* 19 November - [[Nepali Congress]] wins 196 of 575 elected seats in the [[Legislature Parliament of Nepal|Nepalese Constituent Assembly election]]
