{{Year in Nepal|2001}}

Events from the year '''2001 in [[Nepal]]'''.

==Incumbents==
*[[King of Nepal|Monarch]]: 
**[[Birendra of Nepal|Birendra]] (until June 1),
**[[Dipendra of Nepal|Dipendra]] (from June 1 to June 4),
**[[Gyanendra of Nepal|Gyanendra]] (from June 4)
*[[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]]: [[Girija Prasad Koirala]] (until 26 July), [[Sher Bahadur Deuba]] (from 26 July)
*[[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]]: [[Keshav Prasad Upadhyaya]]

==Events==
* January - 10th General Convention of Nepali Congress.
* June 1 - [[Nepalese royal massacre]].
* June 4 - [[Gyanendra of Nepal|Prince Gyanendra]] is crowned King.
* November 23 - Maoists attack a Royal Nepal Army base for the first time in Ghorahi, Dang.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.hrw.org/reports/2008/nepal0908/3.htm |publisher=Human Rights Watch |accessdate=11 May 2019|title=Waiting for Justice: Unpunished Crimes from Nepal's Armed Conflict: II. At War and at Peace }}</ref>
* November 26 - The government declares a national state of emergency.

==Deaths==
* February 5 - [[Daya Bir Singh Kansakar]], social worker
* April 29 - [[Babu Chiri Sherpa]], mountaineer
* June 1 - [[Birendra of Nepal|King Birendra]]
* June 1 - [[Queen Aishwarya of Nepal|Queen Aishwarya]]
* June 1 - [[Princess Shruti of Nepal|Princess Shruti]]
* June 1 - [[Prince Nirajan of Nepal|Prince Nirajan]]
* June 4 - [[Dipendra of Nepal|King Dipendra]]
* November 12 - [[Prekshya Shah|Princess Prekshya]]
