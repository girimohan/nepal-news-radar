{{Year in Nepal|2011}}
Events from the year '''2011 in [[Nepal]]'''.

==Incumbents==
*[[President of Nepal|President]]: [[Ram Baran Yadav]] 
*[[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]]: 
** until 6 February: [[Madhav Kumar Nepal]] 
** 6 February-29 August: [[Jhala Nath Khanal]]
** starting 29 August: [[Baburam Bhattarai]] 
*[[Vice President of Nepal|Vice President]]: [[Parmanand Jha]]
*[[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]]: [[Ram Prasad Shrestha]] (until 5 May), [[Khil Raj Regmi]] (starting 5 May)

==Incumbents==
* 29 August - [[Baburam Bhattarai]] became prime minister.

== Events ==

* In January 2011, [[United Nations|The UN]] mission in Nepal officially ended after disarming [[Maoist rebel]]s to support peace in the country.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Remarks by Representative of the Secretary-General in Nepal Karin Landgren: Flag-lowering ceremony to mark the closure of the mission - Nepal |url=https://reliefweb.int/report/nepal/remarks-representative-secretary-general-nepal-karin-landgren-flag-lowering-ceremony |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260317163917/https://reliefweb.int/report/nepal/remarks-representative-secretary-general-nepal-karin-landgren-flag-lowering-ceremony |archive-date=2026-03-17 |access-date=2026-06-09 |work=ReliefWeb |language=en}}</ref>
* An earthquake known as 2011 [[2011 Sikkim earthquake|Sikkim earthquake]] struck between Sikkim, India, and Nepal and resulted in severe structural damage, power blackouts, and deaths.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2011 |title=Nepal: Earthquake: 2011/09/18 |url=https://www.adrc.asia/view_disaster_en.php?Key=1566 |website=Asian Disaster Reduction center}}</ref>
* In September 2011, a [[Buddha Air Flight 103|Buddha air flight]] crashed in Nepal and resulted in the death of all 19 on the plane,16 tourists and 3 crew members.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Online |first=India Today |date=2011-09-25 |title=10 Indians among 19 killed in Nepal plane crash |url=https://www.indiatoday.in/world/neighbours/story/plane-with-19-people-aboard-missing-in-nepal-141928-2011-09-24 |access-date=2026-05-24 |website=India Today |language=en}}</ref>
* In April 2011, global oil prices spiked due to the [[Libyan crisis]] and caused an acute fuel shortage in Nepal.<ref>{{Cite news |date=2011-04-18 |title=Nepal: Kathmandu hit by acute fuel shortage |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/world-south-asia-13116423 |access-date=2026-05-27 |work=BBC News |language=en-GB}}</ref>
