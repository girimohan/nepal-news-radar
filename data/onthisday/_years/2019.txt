{{Year in Nepal|2019}}

Events in the year '''2019 in [[Nepal]]'''.

==Incumbents==
*[[President of Nepal|President]] : [[Bidhya Devi Bhandari]] 
*[[Vice President of Nepal|Vice President]] : [[Nanda Kishor Pun]]
*[[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]] : [[Khadga Prasad Oli]] 
*[[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]] : [[Om Prakash Mishra (judge)|Om Prakash Mishra]] (until January 1), [[Cholendra Shumsher JBR|Cholendra Shumsher Rana]] (since January 2)
*[[National Assembly (Nepal)|Chairman of National Assembly]] : [[Ganesh Prasad Timilsina]]
*[[House of Representatives (Nepal)|Speaker of House of Representatives]] : [[Krishna Bahadur Mahara]] (until October 1)

* [[Governor (Nepal)|Governor of Sudurpashchim Province]]: [[Mohan Raj Malla]] (starting 19 January and ending 3 November), [[Sharmila Kumari Panta]] (starting 5 November)

==Events==
===January===
* January 3 
** [[Resham Lal Chaudhary]], one of the alleged perpetrators of the [[2015 Tikapur massacre|Tikapur Massacre]], is sworn in as a member of [[House of Representatives (Nepal)|Pratinidhi Sabha]]. He was elected from [[Kailali District|Kailali]] - 1 in the [[2017 Nepalese legislative election]] as a candidate of [[Rastriya Janata Party Nepal]].<ref>{{cite web |title=From the Kathmandu Press: Friday, January 4, 2019 |url=http://english.onlinekhabar.com/from-the-kathmandu-press-friday-january-4-2019.html |website=onlinekhabar |date=4 January 2019 |accessdate=12 January 2019}}</ref>
** Special Court sentences former Additional Inspector General of [[Armed Police Force (Nepal)|Armed Police Force]] Rishav Dev Bhattarai to 9 months in detention for corruption.<ref>{{cite web |title=APF ex-AIG found guilty of corruption |url=https://thehimalayantimes.com/nepal/nepal-armed-police-force-ex-additional-inspector-general-found-guilty-of-corruption/ |website=The Himalayan Times |date=4 January 2019 |publisher=International Media Network Nepal (Pvt) Ltd}}</ref>
* January 8 - [[2018–19 Martyr's Memorial A-Division League]] concludes. [[Manang Marshyangdi Club]] become champions for the eighth time.
* January 9 - [[Govinda K.C.]] begins his 16th hunger strike to protest against the proposed Medical Education Bill.
* January 11 - 16 central committee members of [[Bibeksheel Sajha Party]] leave to form a new party.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Lama |first1=Sanjaya |title=17 months after unification, Bibeksheel Sajha Party splits|url=http://kathmandupost.ekantipur.com/news/2019-01-11/17-months-after-unification-bibeksheel-sajha-party-splits.html |website=ekantipur |publisher=Kantipur Publications Pvt. Ltd. |accessdate=12 January 2019}}</ref>
* January - Convicted felon Rajiv Gurung, alias [[Deepak Manange]], is sworn in as a member of [[Provincial Assembly of Gandaki]]. He was released from prison in December 2018.

===February===
* February 1 - [[Govinda K.C.]] postpones his hunger strike.
* February 6 - [[Supreme Court of Nepal|Supreme Court]] orders government to recover capital gains tax from [[Ncell]] and [[Axiata Group|Axiata]] in relation to the 12 April 2016 sale of Ncell from [[Telia Company]].
* February 14 - [[Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority]] commissioner Raj Narayan Pathak resigns. He is said to have taken 8.7 million rupees as bribe to settle corruption claim in Nepal Engineering College.<ref>{{cite web |title=महाअभियोगबाट जोगिन अख्तियारका आयुक्त राजनारायणद्वारा राजीनामा |url=https://www.onlinekhabar.com/2019/02/742530 |website=Onlinekhabar.com |accessdate=15 February 2019}}</ref>
* February 27 - [[Ministry of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation (Nepal)|Minister of Culture, Tourism Culture and Civil Aviation]] [[Rabindra Adhikari]] is among 7 killed in a [[2019 Taplejung helicopter crash|helicopter crash]].

===March===
* March 6 - [[Rastriya Janata Party Nepal]] parliamentarian Resham Chaudhary is sentenced to life imprisonment for his role in the [[2015 Tikapur massacre|Tikapur Massacre]].
* March 12 - The political party led by Netra Bikram Chand is labeled as a criminal group and its activities banned, leading to nationwide arrests of its members.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Giri |first1=Anil |last2=Pradhan |first2=Tika R |title=Government labels Chand party a criminal group, bans its activities |url=https://kathmandupost.ekantipur.com/news/2019-03-13/government-labels-chand-party-a-criminal-group-bans-its-activities.html |website=Kathmandu Post |accessdate=26 May 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Police arrest key student leader of Chand-led party in capital |url=https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/police-arrest-key-student-leader-of-chand-led-party-in-capital/ |website=Republica}}</ref>
* March 29–30: Nepal Investment submit held in kathmandu.
* March 31 - A [[Nepal tornado|tornado]] kills 28 people in [[Parsa District|Parsa]] and [[Bara District|Bara]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Bidari |first1=Suresh |title=Toll expected to double to 50 in tornado |url=https://www.nepalitimes.com/latest/toll-expected-to-double-to-50-in-tornado/ |website=Nepali Times |accessdate=1 April 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last1=Satyal |first1=Ujjwal|title=What hit Bara, Parsa last week |url=https://thehimalayantimes.com/nepal/what-hit-bara-parsa-last-week/|website=The Himalayan Times |date=7 April 2019 |accessdate=8 April 2019}}</ref> It is the first verified record of a tornado event in the country.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Mallapaty |first1=Smriti |title=Nepali scientists record country's first tornado |url=https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-019-01159-w |journal=Nature |year=2019 |doi=10.1038/d41586-019-01159-w |pmid=32277167 |s2cid=146701694 |accessdate=13 April 2019|url-access=subscription }}</ref>

===April===
* April 14
** Nepali New Year, [[Vikram Samvat|Bikram Sambat 2076]] begins
** A [[Summit Air (Nepal)|Summit Air]] [[Let L-410 Turbolet]] veers off the runway during takeoff at [[Lukla Airport]], impacting a helicopter and killing 3 including the co-pilot.
* April 18 - [[NepaliSat-1]], the first ever research satellite to be sent to space by Nepal launches.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://thehimalayantimes.com/kathmandu/nepalisat-1-to-be-launched-tomorrow/|title=NepaliSat-1 to be launched tomorrow|date=2019-04-17|website=The Himalayan Times|language=en-US|access-date=2019-04-18}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://kathmandupost.ekantipur.com/news/2019-04-18/nepals-first-ever-satellite-launched-into-space.html|title=Nepal's first ever satellite launched into space|website=kathmandupost.ekantipur.com|language=English|access-date=2019-04-18|archive-date=2019-04-18|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190418073044/http://kathmandupost.ekantipur.com/news/2019-04-18/nepals-first-ever-satellite-launched-into-space.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===May===
* May 26 - Four people are killed and seven injured as a result of accidental explosions in [[Kathmandu bombings|three separate incidents]] in Kathmandu. All casualties are reported to be involved with the banned political outfit led by Netra Bikram Chand.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Dhungana |first1=Prakash |title=को हुन् काठमाडौं बम विस्फोटमा मर्ने विप्लवका चार कार्यकर्ता? |url=https://www.setopati.com/politics/181426 |website=Setopati |accessdate=27 May 2019}}</ref>
* May 29 - Finance Minister [[Yuba Raj Khatiwada]] presented the federal budget of Fiscal Year 2019/20 in a joint sitting of both houses of the [[Federal Parliament of Nepal|Federal Parliament]].

===June===
* June 7 - Pranesh Gautam, a youtuber, is arrested for his satirical review of the movie Bir Bikram 2 on the YouTube channel ''memeNepal''.<ref>{{cite web |last1=KC |first1=Masta |title=Scripted arrest |url=https://www.nepalitimes.com/banner/scripted-arrest/ |website=Nepalitime |publisher=Kunda Dixit |accessdate=14 June 2019}}</ref>
* June 18 
** Government withdraws the Guthi Bill tabled in the upper house, ahead of a protest gathering announced for the following day.<ref>{{cite web |title=Street vs Guthi Bill |url=https://www.nepalitimes.com/banner/street-vs-guthi-bill/ |website=Nepalitimes |accessdate=19 June 2019}}</ref>
** A vehicle fell off the road in [[Kharpunath]] gaunpalika, Humla killing 12 including the gaunpalika vice chairman Sindhu Hamal Shahi.
* June 20 - Meteorological Forecasting Division announces that monsoon has entered Nepal, ten days later than the usual date of June 10.<ref>{{cite web |title=Monsoon Enters Nepal, Heavy Rain Likely In Eastern Nepal |url=https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2019/06/20/monsoon-enters-nepal-heavy-rain-likely-eastern-nepal/ |accessdate=1 July 2019}}</ref>

===July===
* July 5 - The cabinet backtracks on its decision to conduct pesticide residue tests on agricultural products entering into the country leading to criticism of the government from even its own party members claiming that it had bowed to Indian pressure.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Khanal |first1=Rajesh |title=Decisions over pesticide tests reveal how the government takes adhoc measures |url=https://kathmandupost.ekantipur.com/news/2019-07-08/decisions-over-pesticide-tests-reveal-how-the-government-takes-uncoordinated-ad-hoc-decisions.html |website=The Kathmandu Post |accessdate=8 July 2019}}</ref>
* July 12–13 - At least 90 people are killed and 29 people are missing due to disasters related to [[2019 Southeast Asia floods|continuous rainfall]].<ref>{{cite tweet |user=NEOCOfficial|number=1151736910462590976 |date= 17 July 2019|title=अविरल वर्षाका कारण भएको मानवीय क्षति एवम् उद्धारको विवरण }}</ref>

===September===
* September 19 - Summer session of parliament ends.

===October===
* October 1 - Speaker of the House of Representatives [[Krishna Bahadur Mahara]] resigns after being accused of rape by a female staff of Parliament.<ref>{{cite web |title=Nepal's parliament speaker resigns after woman alleges rape |url=https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2019/10/nepal-parliament-speaker-resigns-woman-alleges-rape-191001113430984.html |website=Al Jazeera |accessdate=1 October 2019}}</ref>
* October 6 - [[Krishna Bahadur Mahara]] is arrested from the Speaker's residence in Baluwatar for investigation with the rape accusation against him.
* October 12 - Chinese President and General Secretary of the Communist Party [[Xi Jinping]] arrives on a two-day state visit at the invitation of President [[Bidhya Devi Bhandari]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Press Release on state visit of the President of People's Republic of China to Nepal |url=https://mofa.gov.np/press-release-on-state-visit-of-the-president-of-peoples-republic-of-china-to-nepal/ |website=Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Nepal |accessdate=11 October 2019}}</ref>
* October 13 - House of Representative member [[Mohammad Aftab Alam]] of [[Nepali Congress]] is arrested in connection with an explosion and the subsequent murder of at least 23 persons in Rautahat in 2008.<ref name="A">{{cite web |title=The rise and fall of Comrade Mahara |url=https://www.nepalitimes.com/here-now/the-rise-and-fall-of-comrade-mahara/ |website=Nepalitimes |accessdate=17 October 2019}}</ref>
* October 15 - House of Representative member [[Pramod Sah]] of Rastriya Janata Party-Nepal is arrested for vandalism of an airline counter at [[Janakpur Airport]].<ref name="A" />
* October 16 - Police issue an arrest warrant for House of Representative member [[Parbat Gurung]] of [[Nepal Communist Party (NCP)]] on the charge of assault during the local level elections in 2017.<ref name="A" />
* October 24 - Rapper [[VTEN]] is arrested for controversial lyrics in his song ''Hami Yestai Ta Ho Ni Bro''.<ref>{{cite web |title=Kathmandu police arrest rapper VTEN |url=https://english.onlinekhabar.com/kathmandu-police-arrest-rapper-vten.html |website=Onlinekhabar English |date=24 October 2019 |accessdate=25 October 2019}}</ref>

===November===
* November 3 - President Bidhya Devi Bhandari removes all seven provincial governors on recommendation of the Council of Ministers.
* November 5 - President Bidhya Devi Bhandari administers oath of office to new governors of all seven provinces.
* November 9 - Prime Minister K.P Oli convenes an all party meeting to discuss the release of a new political map by India that depicts the disputed [[Kalapani territory|Kalapani]] within India's border.
* November 30 - By-election for vacant seats in [[Federal Parliament of Nepal|Federal Parliament]], [[Provincial Assembly (Nepal)|Provincial Assemblys]] and Local level takes place.

===December===
* December 1 - [[2019 South Asian Games]] begins with an inauguration ceremony in Kathmandu.
* December 10 - [[2019 South Asian Games]] concludes.
* December 20 - Winter session of both houses of [[Federal Parliament of Nepal|Parliament]] begins.
