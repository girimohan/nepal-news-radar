{{Year in Nepal|2018}}

Events in the year '''2018 in [[Nepal]]'''.

==Incumbents==
*[[President of Nepal|President]] : [[Bidhya Devi Bhandari]] 
*[[Vice President of Nepal|Vice President]] : [[Nanda Kishor Pun]]
*[[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]] : [[Sher Bahadur Deuba]] (until February 15), [[Khadga Prasad Oli]] (from February 15)
*[[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]] : [[Gopal Prasad Parajuli]] (until March 14), [[Om Prakash Mishra (judge)|Om Prakash Mishra]] (from August 5)
*[[National Assembly (Nepal)|Chairman of National Assembly]] : [[Ganesh Prasad Timilsina]] (from March 15)
*[[House of Representatives (Nepal)|Speaker of House of Representatives]] : [[Krishna Bahadur Mahara]] (from March 10)

==Events==
===January===
* January 8 - [[Govinda K.C.]] is arrested for [[contempt of court]] after he began his 14th hunger-strike demanding resignation of [[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]] [[Gopal Prasad Parajuli]].
* January 10 - The [[Supreme Court of Nepal|Supreme Court]] releases Govinda K.C. from custody without bail and decides to procure copies of Chief Justice Gopal Prasad Parajuli's two citizenship certificates, Judicial Council's decision on his age controversy and copies of his academic certificates.

===February===
* February 8 - [[National Assembly (Nepal)|National Assembly]] elections takes place.
* February 14 - [[Nepal national cricket team|Nepal]] beats Canada by 1 wicket to secure a spot in [[2018 Cricket World Cup Qualifier]].
* February 15 - Deuba resigns as Prime Minister and Oli is sworn in along with 2 ministers.
* February 26 - Oli expands [[Oli cabinet, 2018|cabinet]] with 4 more ministers.

===March===
* March 4 - Members of the [[Federal parliament of Nepal|Federal Parliament]] are sworn in.
* March 12 - [[US-Bangla Airlines Flight 211]], carrying 67 passengers and 4 crew crashes during landing at [[Tribhuvan International Airport|Kathmandu]] killing 51 on board.
* March 13 - [[Bidhya Devi Bhandari]] is [[2018 Nepalese presidential election|reelected]] as [[President of Nepal|President]].
* March 14 - [[Chief Justice]] [[Gopal Prasad Parajuli]] is removed from office by Judicial Council citing he should have retired on 21 August 2017 citing age bar but administers oath of office to newly elected [[President of Nepal|President]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Judicial Council relieves Chief Justice Parajuli|url=http://kathmandupost.ekantipur.com/news/2018-03-14/judicial-council-removes-parajuli-from-cj-post.html|website=ekantipur|publisher=Kantipur Publications Pvt. Ltd.|accessdate=15 March 2018}}</ref>
* March 15 
** [[Gopal Prasad Parajuli]] announces his resignation to the President even after being removed from the position.
** [[Nepal national cricket team|The national cricket team]] gains [[One Day International]] status after beating Papua New Guinea in the [[2018 Cricket World Cup Qualifier]].
* March 16 - Oli expands [[Oli cabinet, 2018|cabinet]] with 15 more ministers and state ministers, bringing the cabinet to 22 members.

===April===
*April 6 - Prime Minister Oli embarks on a [[state visit]] to [[India]].
*April 11 - [[Sarbendra Khanal]] becomes Inspector General of [[Nepal Police]] following the retirement of [[Prakash Aryal]]. 
*April 14 - Beginning of [[Vikram Samvat|Bikram Sambat]] 2075.
*April 22 - Retired Deputy Inspector General of [[Nepal Police]] Govinda Niraula, who was the former in charge of police in [[Tribhuvan International Airport]], is arrested in connection with the 33&nbsp;kg gold smuggling case.

===May===
*May 2 - Senior Superintendent of [[Nepal Police]] Divesh Lohani is arrested in connection with the 33&nbsp;kg gold smuggling case.<ref>{{cite web|title=SSP Divesh Lohani arrested in connection to smuggling of 33 kg of gold|url=http://setopati.net/social/121649|website=Setopati|accessdate=27 May 2018}}</ref>
*May 6 - Budget session of Federal Parliament begins.
*May 11 - Indian Prime Minister [[Narendra Modi]] arrives on a two-day visit.
*May 13 
** Nepal Electricity Authority declares the nation load shedding free.
** 8 Nepalis make the first Everest summits for this climbing season as part of the rope fixing team.
*May 16 
** [[Makalu Air]] [[Cessna 208B Grand Caravan]] operating a cargo flight crashes in [[Humla]] killing 2 crew members.
** [[Kami Rita|Kami Rita Sherpa]] scales [[Mount Everest]] for a record 22 times.<ref>{{cite news|title=Kami Rita Sherpa scales Mt Everest for record 22 times|url=https://thehimalayantimes.com/nepal/kami-rita-sherpa-scales-mt-everest-record-22-times/|access-date=2023-08-01|website=The Himalayan Times}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|title=Sherpa eyes record-breaking 22nd Everest climb|url=http://gulf-times.com/story/592448/Sherpa-eyes-record-breaking-22nd-Everest-climb|access-date=2023-08-01|website=Gulf Times}}</ref>
*May 22 - Chudamani Upreti alias Gore is arrested in connection with the 33&nbsp;kg gold smuggling case and the murder of Sanam Shakya.<ref>{{cite web|title=गोरे पक्राउ पर्दा क–कसको भयो निद हराम ?|url=http://www.newsofnepal.com/2018/05/22/103681/|website=Nepal Samacharpatra|accessdate=27 May 2018}}</ref>
*May 29 - Finance Minister [[Yuba Raj Khatiwada]] presented the federal budget of Fiscal Year 2018/19 in a joint sitting of both houses of the Federal Parliament.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://sharesansar.com/newsdetail/finance-minister-dr-khatiwada-unveils-fiscal-budget-of-fy-207576-live-video |website=Sharesansar|title=Finance Minister Dr. Khatiwada unveils fiscal budget of FY 2075/76 [LIVE VIDEO]
 |date=15 December 2016|accessdate=31 May 2018}}</ref>
*May 31 - Senior Superintendent of [[Nepal Police]] Shyam Khatri surrenders himself to Morang District Court in connection with the 33&nbsp;kg gold smuggling case.

===June===
*June 19 - Prime Minister Oli embarks on a 6-day official visit to the [[People's Republic of China]].

===July===
*July 6 - Provincial lawmakers pass a resolution to name Province No. 4 as [[Gandaki Province|Gandaki]].
*July 24 - Law Minister [[Sher Bahadur Tamang]] resigns after making controversial remarks regarding female medical students in Bangladesh.
*July 27 - Thirteen year old [[Nirmala Pant]] is raped and murdered in [[Bhimdatta]], [[Kanchanpur District|Kanchanpur]] leading to nationwide protests.<ref>{{cite web |title=Nationwide protests today in support of Nirmala |url=https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/nationwide-protests-today-in-support-of-nirmala/ |website=myRepública |publisher=Nepal Republic Media |accessdate=3 November 2018}}</ref>

===August===
*August 30–31 - The fourth summit of [[Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation|BIMSTEC]] takes place in [[Kathmandu]].

===September===
*September 8 - [[Altitude Air]] helicopter crashes in [[Dhading District|Dhading]] killing 6 of the 7 people on board.
*September 9 - [[Purna Chandra Thapa]] becomes the new Chief of Army Staff of [[Nepalese Army|Nepal Army]] following the retirement of [[Rajendra Chhetri]].
*September 27 - Budget session of Federal Parliament ends.
*September 28 - Provincial lawmakers pass a resolution to name Province No. 7 as [[Sudurpashchim Province|Sudurpashchim]].

===October===
*October 13 - Five South Korean climbers and four Nepali guides die after their camp is hit by an avalanche air blast. They were planning to ascent the south face of [[Gurja Himal]].<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-45848535|title = Nepal storm kills climbers on Himalayan peak Gurja|work = BBC News|date = 14 October 2018}}</ref>
*October 25 - Superintendent Dilli Raj Bista and Inspector Jagdish Bhatta of Nepal Police are removed from service due to their roles in the mishandling of the Nirmala Pant rape and murder case.

===November===
*November 2 - Physical Infrastructure and Development Minister of Province no.3 [[Keshav Sthapit]] is removed from his post after allegations of sexual assaults and alleged misbehaviour with Chief Minister [[Dormani Poudel]].
*November 6–10 people are killed after a vehicle falls into [[Bheri River|Bheri]] in [[Dolpa District|Dolpa]].<ref>{{cite web |title=डोल्पामा अहिलेसम्म कै ठूलो सडक दुर्घटना |url=http://annapurnapost.com/news/112245 |website=Annapurna Post |publisher=Annapurna Media Network |accessdate=6 November 2018}}</ref>
*November 22 - 18 people are killed in a bus accident in [[Dadeldhura District|Dadeldhura]].<ref>{{cite web |title=मेलाबाट घर फर्किएका यात्रु बोकेको बस डडेलधुरामा दुर्घटना, १६ को मृत्यु |url=http://pahilopost.com/content/20181122215649.html |website=Pahilopost |publisher=Bizmandu Pvt. Ltd. |accessdate=23 November 2018}}</ref>

===December===
*December 7 - [[Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority]] files charges against 21 people, including former minister Bikram Pandey, accusing them of corruption on [[Sikta Irrigation Project]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Rs 2b corruption case against ex-minister Bikram Pandey over Sikta |url=http://setopati.net/social/135040 |website=Setopati |accessdate=8 December 2018}}</ref>
*December 9–10 - The first Inter province council meeting takes place in [[Kathmandu]].
*December 14 - A truck accident in [[Dupcheshwar Rural Municipality]], [[Nuwakot District|Nuwakot]] kills 20 people.
*December 20 - [[Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority]] files charges against 3 people, including former governor of [[Nepal Rastra Bank]] Tilak Rawal, accusing them of corruption during the printing of polymer notes.
*December 21 - A bus accident in [[Tulsipur, Dang|Tulsipur]], [[Dang Deukhuri District|Dang]] kills 23 people. The victims were schoolchildren and teachers returning from a field trip.
*December 26 - Winter session of Federal Parliament begins.

==Deaths==
*January 26 &ndash; [[Elizabeth Hawley]],  journalist and Himalayan expedition historian
*March 6 &ndash; [[Indra Bahadur Rai]], writer and literary critic
*June 18 &ndash; [[Upendra Devkota]], [[neurosurgeon]] and former [[Ministry of Health and Population (Nepal)|Minister of Health, Science and Technology]]
*July 17 &ndash; [[Tara Subba]], [[Mayor]] of [[Dharan, Nepal|Dharan]]
*August 10 &ndash; [[Bharat Shumsher]], opposition leader in Nepal's first parliament
*November 4 &ndash; [[Padma Ratna Tuladhar]], politician and human rights activist
*December 18 &ndash; [[Tulsi Giri]], former Prime Minister
*December 28 &ndash; Buddhi Krishna Lamichhame, folk singer
