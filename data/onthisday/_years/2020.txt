==Events==
===January===
*January 1 - [[Visit Nepal 2020]] officially begins with an inauguration program at [[Dasarath Rangasala Stadium|Dasarath Rangasala]].
*January 21 - 8 Indian tourists die as a result of [[Carbon Monoxide]] poisoning at a resort in [[Daman, Nepal|Daman]], [[Makwanpur District|Makwanpur]].<ref>{{cite web |title=8 Indian tourists die after falling unconscious at Nepal hotel |url=https://www.channelnewsasia.com/news/asia/8-indian-tourists-die-after-falling-unconscious-at-nepal-hotel-12296696 |website=CNA |access-date=22 January 2020 |archive-date=21 January 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200121154237/https://www.channelnewsasia.com/news/asia/8-indian-tourists-die-after-falling-unconscious-at-nepal-hotel-12296696 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
*January 23 - [[2020 Nepalese National Assembly election|Elections]] for 18 Class I seats in the [[National Assembly (Nepal)|National Assembly]] takes place.<ref>{{cite web |title=Voting over to elect 18 National Assembly members |url=https://english.onlinekhabar.com/voting-underway-to-elect-18-national-assembly-members.html |website=Onlinekhabar English |date=23 January 2020 |access-date=25 January 2020}}</ref>
*January 24 - The first case of [[COVID-19]] in Nepal is confirmed by the health ministry.<ref>{{cite web |title=Nepal confirms first case of new coronavirus infection |url=http://www.xinhuanet.com/english/2020-01/25/c_138732435.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200125093406/http://www.xinhuanet.com/english/2020-01/25/c_138732435.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=January 25, 2020 |website=Xinhua |access-date=25 January 2020}}</ref>

===February===
*February 5 - [[Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority]] files corruption case in Special Court against 175 persons, including 3 former ministers, for their involvement in illegally privatizing government land of Lalita Niwas, [[Baluwatar, Kathmandu|Baluwatar]].<ref>{{cite web |title=ललितानिवास जग्गा प्रकरणमा को को विरुद्ध मुद्दा ? |url=https://www.onlinekhabar.com/2020/02/834020 |website=Onlinekhabar |access-date=5 February 2020}}</ref>
*February 12 - [[Thakur Prasad Gyawaly]] becomes the [[Inspector General of Police (Nepal)|Inspector General]] of [[Nepal Police]] following the retirement of [[Sarbendra Khanal]].<ref>{{cite web |title=ठाकुर ज्ञवाली प्रहरी महानिरीक्षक नियुक्त |url=https://www.setopati.com/social/199504 |website=Setopati |access-date=11 February 2020}}</ref>
*February 20 - Government spokesperson and Minister [[Gokul Prasad Baskota]] resigns after an audio tape arranging kickbacks in procurement of secure printing press from [[Koenig & Bauer|KBA-NotaSys SA]].<ref>{{cite web |title=बाँस्कोटाको राजीनामासँगै दुई मन्त्रालय खाली |url=https://www.setopati.com/politics/200070 |website=Setopati |access-date=20 February 2020}}</ref>
*February 29 - Visit Nepal 2020 campaign is suspended due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic|COVID-19 outbreak]].

===March===
*March 15 - A meeting is held between the high level committee for prevention and control of coronavirus and all seven provincial chief ministers to discuss the ongoing pandemic.<ref>{{cite web |title=कोरोनाभाइरसबारे छलफल गर्न मुख्यमन्त्रीहरू काठमाडौंमा |url=https://www.setopati.com/politics/201553 |website=Setopati |access-date=15 March 2020}}</ref>
*March 20 - Prime Minister Oli addresses the nation regarding the ongoing [[2020 coronavirus pandemic in Nepal|coronavirus pandemic]]. All international flights are stopped from March 22 to March 31.<ref>{{cite web |title=देशभित्र यातायात बन्द, भारतीय सीमा नाका खुल्ला ! |url=https://www.onlinekhabar.com/2020/03/846036 |website=Onlinekhabar |access-date=21 March 2020}}</ref>
*March 24 - Nationwide lock-down to stop the spread of [[COVID-19]] begins.<ref>{{cite web |title=आज बिहानैबाट मुलुकभर लक डाउन, नटेर्नेलाई छ महिनासम्मको कैद |url=https://pahilopost.com/content/20200324001755.html |website=Pahilopost |access-date=24 March 2020}}</ref>

===April===
*April 6 - Fifth session of [[Federal Parliament of Nepal|Federal Parliament]] ends.
*April 13 - Nepali New Year, [[Vikrama Samvat|Bikram Sambat 2077]] begins.
*April 21 - Two ordinances amending Constitutional Council (Functions, Duties, Powers and Procedures) Act, 2066 and Political Parties Act, 2073 are endorsed.<ref>{{cite web |title=President Bhandari endorses two ordinances |url=https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/president-bhandari-endorses-two-ordinances/ |website=Republica |access-date=21 April 2020}}</ref>
*April 22 - [[Samajbadi Party, Nepal (2019)|Samajbadi Party, Nepal]] and [[Rastriya Janata Party Nepal]] announce a merger to form [[People's Socialist Party, Nepal (2020)|People's Socialist Party, Nepal]] as a response to the April 21st ordinance.<ref>{{cite web |title=जनता समाजवादी पार्टी दर्ताका लागि निर्वाचन आयोगमा निवेदन |url=https://ekantipur.com/news/2020/04/23/15876219547717248.html |website=Ekantipur |publisher=Kailash Shirohiya |access-date=23 April 2020}}</ref> Breakaway factions in [[Samajbadi Party, Nepal (2019)|Samajbadi Party, Nepal]] under the leadership of [[Renu Yadav]] were maneuvering to split the party as per the new provisions in Political Parties Act, 2073.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Bagale |first1=Sanjeev |title=रेणु यादवको नेतृत्वमा समाजवादी पार्टी फुटाउने सात सांसदको निर्णय |url=https://www.setopati.com/politics/204577 |website=Setopati |access-date=23 April 2020}}</ref>
*April 24 - Two ordinances passed on April 21 are repealed by the President following the recommendation of the Council of Ministers.<ref>{{cite web |title=सरकारले ल्याएका अध्यादेश राष्ट्रपतिबाट खारेज |url=https://www.onlinekhabar.com/2020/04/857911 |website=Onlinekhabar |access-date=24 April 2020}}</ref> The endorsement of the ordinances were criticized both within the ruling party and other opposition parties.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Pradhan |first1=Tika R. |last2=Ghimire |first2=BInod |title=Cabinet decides to recommend repealment of two ordinances that were promulgated on Monday |url=https://kathmandupost.com/politics/2020/04/24/cabinet-decides-to-recommend-repealment-of-two-ordinances-that-were-promulgated-on-monday |website=The Kathmandu Post |access-date=24 April 2020}}</ref>

===May===
*May 7 - The number of total COVID-19 infections exceeds 100.<ref>{{cite web |title=Two more Covid-19 cases take Nepal's tally to 101 |url=https://kathmandupost.com/health/2020/05/07/two-more-covid-19-cases-take-nepal-s-tally-to-101 |website=Kathmandu Post |publisher=Kailash Sirohiya |access-date=9 May 2020}}</ref>
*May 8 - Sixth session of [[Federal Parliament of Nepal|Federal Parliament]] begins.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Rai |first1=Saindra |title=Parliament as lightning rod |url=https://www.nepalitimes.com/latest/parliament-as-lightning-rod/ |website=Nepalitimes |date=22 February 2018 |publisher=Kunda Dixit |access-date=7 May 2020}}</ref>
*May 11 - The government calls on the Indian ambassador to convey its position and hand over a diplomatic note following the inauguration of a road to link with China at [[Lipulekh pass]] which lies in the disputed region of [[Kalapani territory|Kalapani]].<ref>{{cite web |title=भारतीय राजदूत विनयमोहनलाई सरकारले दियो 'कूटनीतिक नोट' |url=https://www.prasashan.com/2020/05/11/178190/ |website=Prasashan |access-date=11 May 2020}}</ref>
*May 16 - Ministry of Health and Population confirms the first death from COVID-19 in the country; the deceased 29-year-old woman had recently given birth on May 6 at [[Tribhuvan University Teaching Hospital|Teaching Hospital]], [[Kathmandu]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Poudel |first1=Arjun |title=Nepal reports its first Covid-19 death |url=https://kathmandupost.com/national/2020/05/16/no-evidence-yet-that-covid-19-patient-s-death-on-thursday-was-due-to-the-disease |website=Kathmandu Post |publisher=Kailash Sirohiya |access-date=17 May 2020}}</ref>
*May 18 - Cabinet approves a new official map of the country which extends the north-western boundary up to Limpiyadhura adding 335 square kilometres in area to the existing map.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Subedi |first1=Binu |title=लिम्पियाधुरासहित ३३५ वर्ग किलोमिटर भूमि अब नेपालको नक्सामा |url=https://ekantipur.com/news/2020/05/19/158985055994146227.html |website=Ekantipur |access-date=19 May 2020}}</ref>
*May 23 - 6 people are killed in [[Chaurjahari]], [[Western Rukum District|Western Rukum]] after a mob [[Soti incident|attacks Nawaraj BK and his friends]], who had allegedly come to take BK's 17-year-old girlfriend for marriage.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Singh |first1=Bhim Bahadur |title=Soti incident: Sixth missing body found after 10 days |url=https://kathmandupost.com/karnali-province/2020/06/02/soti-incident-sixth-missing-body-found-after-10-days |website=Kathmandu Post |publisher=Kailash Sirohiya |access-date=13 June 2020}}</ref>  
*May 28 
** The number of total COVID-19 infections crosses 1000.<ref>{{cite web |title=थप १५६ जनामा संक्रमण पुष्टि, संक्रमितको संख्या १०४२ पुग्यो |url=https://ekantipur.com/news/2020/05/28/15906557175901258.html |website=Ekantipur |publisher=Kailash Sirohiya |access-date=28 May 2020}}</ref>
** Finance Minister [[Yuba Raj Khatiwada]] presents the federal budget of NRs. 1.47 trillion for Fiscal Year 2020/21 to a joint sitting of both houses of parliament.

===June===
*June 18 - The second amendment to the [[Constitution of Nepal]] is authenticated by the President after unanimous approval in the [[National Assembly (Nepal)|National Assembly]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Bajgain |first1=Raghunath |title=राष्ट्रपतिद्वारा संविधान संशोधन विधेयक प्रमाणीकरण, नयाँ नक्साले पायो संवैधानिक मान्यता |url=https://www.onlinekhabar.com/2020/06/874437 |website=Onlinekhabar |access-date=18 June 2020}}</ref>
*June 21 - [[Solar eclipse of June 21, 2020|Solar Eclipse]] is visible from all parts of the country.
*June 23 - The number of total COVID-19 infections crosses 10,000.<ref>{{cite web |title=नेपालमा १० हजार नाघ्यो कोरोना संक्रमित, मंगलवार ५३८ मा कोरोना पुष्टि |url=https://pahilopost.com/content/20200623162806.html |website=Pahilopost |access-date=23 June 2020}}</ref>
*June 27 - [[Locust]]s are reported to have entered into the country.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Punthoki |first1=Prem |title=सलह नेपाल भित्रियो |url=https://nagariknews.nagariknetwork.com/social-affairs/255824-1593240737.html |website=Nagarik |publisher=Binod Raj Gyawali |access-date=27 June 2020}}</ref> See: [[2019–20 locust infestation]]

===July===
*July 2 - The President abruptly prorogues the sixth session of [[Federal Parliament of Nepal|Federal Parliament]] on the basis of the recommendation of the government.<ref>{{cite web |title=राष्ट्रपति भण्डारीद्वारा संसद् अधिवेशन अन्त्य |url=https://ekantipur.com/news/2020/07/02/159367887664238781.html |website=Ekantipur |publisher=Kailash Sirohiya |access-date=2 July 2020}}</ref>
* July 9 - 2 people are dead and 21 missing after landslides in [[Bahrabise]] and [[Bhotekoshi]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://ujyaaloonline.com/story/39986/2020/7/9/landslide-in-sindhupalchowk|title=सिन्धुपाल्चोक पहिरो अपडेट : खहरे खोलाको बाढीले २२ जना बेपत्ता, झण्डै दुई दर्जन घर बगायो|website=Ujyaalo Online}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://drrportal.gov.np/ |website=Nepal Disaster Risk Reduction Portal|title=Data |access-date=16 July 2020}}</ref>
* July 10–14 - 60 dead and 41 missing after [[2020 Nepal floods|floods]] in Western Nepal.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/south-asia/nepal-60-dead-41-missing-in-floods-landslides/articleshow/76934877.cms|title=Nepal: 60 dead, 41 missing in floods, landslides - Times of India|website=The Times of India|date=13 July 2020 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.dw.com/en/floods-and-landslides-lash-nepal-scores-dead/a-54142287|title=Floods and landslides lash Nepal, scores dead &#124; DW &#124; 12.07.2020|website=DW.COM}}</ref>
* July 22 - Nationwide lockdown ends.<ref>{{cite web |title= Nepal ends COVID-19 lockdown/ |url= https://www.nepalitimes.com/latest/nepal-ends-covid-19-lockdown|website=Nepali Times |date= 21 July 2020|publisher=Kunda Dixit |access-date=15 August 2020}}</ref>
* July 27 - [[Lumpy skin disease outbreak in Nepal|Outbreak of lumpy skin disease]] is confirmed and declared.<ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Acharya |first1=Krishna Prasad |last2=Subedi |first2=Deepak |year=2020|title=First outbreak of lumpy skin disease in Nepal |journal=Transboundary and Emerging Diseases |language=en |volume=67 |issue=6 |pages=2280–2281 |doi=10.1111/tbed.13815 |pmid=32889764 |issn=1865-1674|doi-access=free }}</ref>

===August===
*August 15 - Ministry of Health and Population confirms the hundredth death from COVID-19 in the country.<ref>{{cite web |title=वीरगञ्जमा थप एक कोरोना संक्रमितको मृत्यु, मृतकको संख्या १०० पुग्यो |url=https://swasthyakhabar.com/story/34251 |website=Swasthyakhabar |access-date=15 August 2020}}</ref>
*August 18 - Daily cases of COVID-19 exceeds 1000.<ref>{{cite web |title=एकै दिन १०१६ जनामा कोरोना संक्रमण पुष्टि |url=https://www.onlinekhabar.com/2020/08/890389 |website=Onlinekhabar |access-date=18 August 2020}}</ref>

===September===
*September 13 - [[Kul Man Ghising]] completes his four-year term as [[Managing Director]] of [[Nepal Electricity Authority]], having successfully eliminated [[Rolling blackout|Load shedding]] from the entire country during his tenure.

===October===
*October 3 - Three key advisors and private secretary to Prime Minister Oli test positive for COVID-19.<ref>{{cite news  |title=Nepal Prime Minister's advisers test positive for COVID-19 |url=https://www.reuters.com/article/us-health-coronavirus-nepal/three-advisers-to-nepal-pm-oli-test-positive-for-covid-19-idUSKBN26O0ML?feedType=mktg&feedName=worldNews&WT.mc_id=Partner-Google |website=Reuters |date=3 October 2020 |access-date=4 October 2020}}</ref> More than 60 security personnel in the [[Baluwatar, Kathmandu|Prime ministerial residence]] also test positive for COVID-19.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Acharya |first1=Swarup |title=बालुवाटारमा कार्यरत दर्जनौं संक्रमित, प्रधानमन्त्रीलाई भने जोखिम नभएको दाबी |url=https://ekantipur.com/news/2020/10/03/160173648858399286.html |website=Ekantipur |publisher=Sirohiaya, Kailash |access-date=4 October 2020}}</ref>
*October 4 - [[Provincial Assembly of Lumbini Province|Provincial Assembly]] pass a resolution to name Province No. 5 as [[Lumbini Province|Lumbini]] with [[Deukhuri]] as its capital.

===November===
*November 4 - Ministry of Health and Population confirms the one thousandth death from COVID-19 in the country.

===December===
*December 20 - President dissolves the [[House of Representatives (Nepal)|House of Representatives]] on the recommendation of the Cabinet.<ref>{{cite web |title=प्रधानमन्त्रीद्वारा संसद विघटन गर्न सिफारिस |url=https://www.onlinekhabar.com/2020/12/916906 |website=Onlinekhabar |access-date=20 December 2020}}</ref> Seven ministers resign in protest.<ref>{{cite web |title=Seven ministers, all from the Dahal-Nepal faction in ruling party, resign |url=https://kathmandupost.com/politics/2020/12/20/seven-ministers-all-from-the-dahal-nepal-faction-in-ruling-party-resign-1608463576 |website=Kathmandu Post |publisher=Kailash Sirohiya |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref>
*December 22 - Parallel central committee meetings of [[Nepal Communist Party|Nepal Communist Party (NCP)]] takes place.<ref>{{cite web |title=Nepal selected new Nepal Communist Party chair to replace Oli|url=https://kathmandupost.com/politics/2020/12/22/nepal-selected-new-nepal-communist-party-chair-to-replace-oli |website=Kathmandu Post |publisher=Kailash Sirohiya |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref>
