The following lists events that happened during '''[[1959]] in the [[Kingdom of Nepal]]'''.

==Incumbents==
*[[King of Nepal|Monarch]]: [[Mahendra of Nepal|Mahendra]]
*[[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]]: [[Subarna Shamsher Rana]] (until 27 May), [[Bishweshwar Prasad Koirala]] (starting 27 May)
*[[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]]: [[Anirudra Prasad Singh]] (until 29 June)

==Events==
===February===
* February 18 - [[1959 Nepalese legislative election|Elections were held in Nepal]] for the first time its history, as voters chose candidates for 18 of the 109 lower house seats, with the remainder to be chosen on eight other days.<ref>"Nepal Casts Vote For First Time", ''Oakland Tribune'', February 18, 1959, p2</ref>
