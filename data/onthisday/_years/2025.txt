== Events ==
=== January ===
* 7 January – A [[2025 Xizang earthquake|magnitude 7.1 earthquake]] with an epicentre in neighbouring [[Tibet]] shakes parts of Nepal, injuring at least 13 people across the country.<ref>{{cite news|title=Powerful earthquake rocks remote region of Tibet and parts of Nepal, killing more than 120 |url=https://edition.cnn.com/2025/01/06/china/china-tibet-earthquake-intl-hnk/index.html |access-date=7 January 2025|work=[[CNN]] |date=7 January 2025}}</ref>
* 15 January – The [[Supreme Court of Nepal]] strikes down several laws that allowed for the construction of hydropower and hotel projects in protected nature reserves.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nepal's top court bars infrastructure in protected areas |url=https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20250116-nepal-s-top-court-bars-infrastructure-in-protected-areas |access-date=16 January 2025 |website=France 24 |date=16 January 2025 |language=en}}</ref>

=== February ===
* 27 February – A magnitude 5.5 earthquake hits [[Bagmati Province]], injuring six people.<ref>{{cite news|title=भूकम्प अपडेट: काठमाडौँमा २ सहित ६ जना घाइते, ६ घर-गोठ र प्रहरी कार्यालयमा क्षति|url=https://thahakhabar.com/news/222502/|language=ne|date=28 February 2025|access-date=28 February 2025|website=Thaha Khabar}}</ref>

=== March  ===
* 28 March – Two people, including a journalist, are killed during clashes between police and [[King of Nepal|monarchist]] demonstrators in [[Kathmandu]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Journalist, protester killed at Nepal pro-monarchy rally |url=https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20250328-nepal-police-clash-with-pro-monarchy-demonstrators |access-date=28 March 2025 |website=France 24 |date=28 March 2025 |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Ellis-Petersen |first=Hannah |date=2025-03-28 |title=Two killed in Kathmandu rally demanding return of Nepal monarchy |url=https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/mar/28/kathmandu-rally-demanding-restoration-nepal-monarchy |access-date=2025-04-01 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref>

=== April ===
* 4 April – A magnitude 4.9 earthquake hits [[Karnali Province]], injuring eight people.<ref>{{cite news|title=तीन मिनेट फरकमा दुईपटक भूकम्प: ८ जना घाइते, ३ जिल्लामा घर तथा सरकारी भवन चर्किए|trans-title=Two Earthquakes Within Three Minutes: 8 Injured, Houses and Government Buildings Cracked in 3 Districts |url=https://nepalkhabar.com/society/232302-2025-4-5-13-25-25|date=5 April 2025|access-date=5 April 2025|lang=ne|publisher=Nepal Khabar}}</ref>
* 9 April – Heavy rain and storms cause widespread damage, with at least eight deaths reported nationwide.<ref>{{Cite web |last1=Dash |first1=Jatindra |last2=Sharma |first2=Gopal |date=11 April 2025 |title=More than 100 people killed after heavy rain hits India, Nepal |url=https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/climate-energy/nearly-100-killed-after-heavy-rain-india-nepal-2025-04-11/ |access-date=16 April 2025 |website=Reuters}}</ref>
* 28 April – An Austrian climber is reported dead on [[Ama Dablam]] after going missing on 26 April.<ref>{{Cite web |date=28 April 2025 |title=Austrian climber dies on Nepal mountain |url=https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20250428-austrian-climber-dies-on-nepal-mountain |access-date=28 April 2025 |website=France 24}}</ref>

=== May ===
* 4 May – An American climber dies after suffering a [[cardiac arrest]] while descending from [[Makalu]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=7 May 2025 |title=American climber dies while attempting to scale Nepal's Mount Makalu |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-mountaineering-climber-died-c25175b41a9c4203313584f33a344528 |access-date=7 May 2025 |website=AP News}}</ref>

=== June ===
* 10 June – The [[European Union]] adds Nepal to its list of high risk jurisdictions for money laundering and terrorism financing.<ref>{{cite news |title=EU removes Philippines from 'high-risk' money-laundering list, adds Monaco |url=https://www.abs-cbn.com/news/business/2025/6/12/eu-removes-philippines-from-high-risk-money-laundering-list-adds-monaco-1432 |access-date=June 12, 2025 |publisher=[[ABS-CBN]] |date=June 12, 2025}}</ref>

=== July ===
* 8 July – The Friendship Bridge connecting Nepal's [[Rasuwa District]] with China is swept away due to flooding along the [[Bhotekoshi River]], killing nine people and leaving 19 others missing.<ref>{{cite news |title=Rescuers search for 19 missing and recover 9 bodies after flooding in Nepal |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-flooding-bridge-china-border-013b27f79752be8138763842600450ac |access-date=9 July 2025 |publisher=[[AP News]] |date=9 July 2025}}</ref>

=== August ===

* 17 August – Heavy [[Monsoon|monsoon rains]] trigger sudden floods and [[landslides]]s nationwide, killing at least 41 people and injuring 121 others, according to the national disaster management authority.<ref>{{Cite web |author1=Sophia Saifi |author2=Rhea Mogul |author3=Sana Noor Haq |date=2025-08-15 |title=Sudden, heavy rain in Pakistan, India-administered Kashmir and Nepal kills more than 200 people |url=https://www.cnn.com/2025/08/15/india/india-kashmir-cloudburst-rain-pilgrimage-intl-hnk |access-date=2025-08-19 |website=CNN |language=en}}</ref>
* 27 August – The [[Supreme Court of Nepal]] rejects a petition to bar [[Daśanāmi Sampradaya#Naga Sadhus|naga sadhus]] from entering the [[Pashupatinath Temple]] and other [[Shaivism|Shaivite temples]], saying that nudity in the name of religion cannot be regarded as obscene.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-08-27 |title=Nudity in devotion is not obscenity, Nepal Supreme Court rules |url=https://kathmandupost.com/national/2025/08/27/nudity-in-devotion-is-not-obscenity-nepal-supreme-court-rules |access-date=2025-09-07 |website=The Kathmandu Post |language=en}}</ref>

=== September ===

* 4 September – The government orders the blocking of 24 social media platforms including [[Facebook]], [[Twitter|X]] and [[YouTube]] for failing to register with it.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gurubacharya |first=Binaj |date=2025-09-04 |title=Nepal blocks Facebook, X, YouTube and others for failing to register with the government |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-ban-social-media-platform-3b42bbbd07bc9b97acb4df09d42029d5 |access-date=2025-09-04 |website=AP News |language=en}}</ref>
* 8 September – [[2025 Nepalese Gen Z protests|Nationwide protests]] are carried out by youths primarily against the social media ban and corruption.<ref>{{Cite web | date=2025-09-08 | title= Gen Z protest in Kathmandu against corruption and ban on social media platforms | url=https://kathmandupost.com/visual-stories/2025/09/08/gen-z-protest-in-kathmandu-against-corruption-and-social-media-ban | access-date=2025-09-08 | website=The Kathmandu Post | language=en}}</ref> Clashes with police initially kill at least 72 people and injure over 2,100.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Sharma |first=Gopal |date=2025-09-14 |title=Death toll from Nepal's anti-corruption protests raised to 72 |url=https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/death-toll-nepals-anti-corruption-protests-raised-72-2025-09-14/ |access-date=2025-09-15 |work=Reuters |language=en}}</ref> The unrest prompts the resignation of Home Minister [[Ramesh Lekhak]]<ref>{{cite news |date=8 September 2025 |title=Nepal Social media protest: Home minister Ramesh Lekhak resigns from post after worst violence; 19 dead, several injured |url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/south-asia/nepal-social-media-protest-home-minister-ramesh-lekhak-resigns-from-post-after-worst-violence-19-dead-several-injured/articleshow/123768848.cms |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20250908155852/https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/south-asia/nepal-social-media-protest-home-minister-ramesh-lekhak-resigns-from-post-after-worst-violence-19-dead-several-injured/articleshow/123768848.cms |archivedate=8 September 2025 |accessdate=8 September 2025 |publisher=The Times of India}}</ref> and the lifting of the social media ban.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-09-09 |title=Nepal’s prime minister resigns as protests against the government and corruption rage on |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-protest-social-media-ban-89cf500969536cf2a35c3fb884cfa620 |access-date=2025-09-09 |website=AP News |language=en}}</ref>
* 9 September – 
**[[K. P. Sharma Oli]] resigns as prime minister following the [[2025 Nepalese Gen Z protests]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Nepal PM KP Oli resigns after violent anti-corruption protests: Report|url=https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/nepal-pm-kp-oli-resigns-after-violent-anti-corruption-protests-report-101757407595025.html |website=[[Hindustan Times]] |date=2025-09-09 |access-date=2025-09-09}}</ref>
**The headquarters of the [[Federal Parliament of Nepal]], multiple government buildings and the residences of leading politicians in [[Kathmandu]] are set on fire by [[2025 Nepalese Gen Z protests|antigovernment protesters]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-09-09 |title=Nepal's PM resigns after violent protests over social media ban, government corruption spread |url=https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/nepal-protests-grow-social-media-ban-politicians-houses-set-fire-1.7628689 |access-date=2025-09-09 |website=CBC |language=en}}</ref>
* 12 September – 
**Former chief justice [[Sushila Karki]] is appointed as interim prime minister, making her the first woman to hold the office.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-09-12 |title=Nepal ex-chief justice Sushila Karki named as next PM |url=https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20250912-nepal-seeks-new-leader-as-army-reclaims-streets-after-protest-violence |access-date=2025-09-12 |website=France 24 |language=en}}</ref>
**President Poudel dissolves parliament following the recommendation of interim prime minister Karki in preparation for elections due on 5 March 2026.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-09-13 |title=Nepal's major parties say dissolved parliament must be reinstated |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0q71jplqq0o |access-date=2025-09-16 |website=BBC |language=en}}</ref>
* 22 September – Polish climber [[Andrzej Bargiel]] becomes the first person to ski down [[Mount Everest]] without the use of supplemental [[oxygen]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-09-25 |title=In a first, Polish climber skis down Everest without supplemental oxygen |url=https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/9/25/in-a-first-polish-climber-skis-down-everest-without-supplemental-oxygen |access-date=2025-09-25 |website=Al Jazeera |language=en}}</ref>
* 30 September – Two-year-old Aryatara Shakya is installed as the new [[Kumari Devi]] in [[Kathmandu]], succeeding Trishna Shakya who held the position since 2017.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nepal chooses a 2-year-old girl as new living goddess worshipped by both Hindus and Buddhists |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-living-goddess-hindu-tradition-0cd93fa79e3446ffdf995210f44b8f99 |last=Gurubacharya |first=Binaj |date=2025-09-30 |access-date=2025-10-02 |website=[[Associated Press]]}}</ref>

===October===
* 5 October – At least 44 people are reported killed in nationwide landslides and flooding caused by heavy rains.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-10-06 |title=Nepalese villages are swept away by heavy rainfall and landslides, leaving at least 44 dead |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-rainfall-monsoon-landslide-deaths-1142e1dccb9772a6fa199d62a627e777 |access-date=2025-10-06 |website=AP News |language=en}}</ref>
* 7 October – A South Korean climber is killed in a storm on [[Mera Peak]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://apnews.com/article/china-nepal-everest-climber-hikers-stranded-trapped-342f3f2672073badf828384dc9768e12 |title=Snowstorms strand hikers in China and a South Korean climber dies in Nepal |website=[[Associated Press]]|access-date=2025-10-07 |date=2025-10-09}}</ref>
* 20 October – The remains of Bipin Joshi, a student who died in captivity in the Gaza Strip after being abducted by [[Hamas]] in the [[7 October attacks]], are repatriated to Nepal.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-israel-hostage-body-repatriated-d8189af5070f73f234c0aaab8f1fc5fb |title=Body of slain Nepali hostage held in Gaza repatriated after release by Hamas |website=[[Associated Press]]|access-date=2025-10-20 |date=2025-10-20 }}</ref>

===November===
* 3 November – Seven climbers, including five foreign nationals, are killed in an avalanche on Mount Yalung Ri.<ref>{{cite news|title=Avalanche on Nepal mountain Yalung Ri kills 7 including foreign climbers |url=https://apnews.com/article/nepal-avalanche-yalung-ri-deaths-mountain-d80c323fd21acb42ea9b5211481d26ce |date=3 November 2025|access-date=3 November 2025|work=[[AP News]]}}</ref>
