{{Year in Nepal|2005}}
Events from the year '''2005 in [[Nepal]]'''.

==Incumbents==
*[[King of Nepal|Monarch]]: [[Gyanendra of Nepal|Gyanendra]]
*[[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]]: [[Sher Bahadur Deuba]] (until 1 February)
*[[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]]:  
** Until 13 January: [[Govinda Bahadur Shrestha]]
** 14 January - 29 July: [[Hari Prasad Sharma]]
** Starting 31 July: [[Dilip Kumar Poudel]]

==Events==
* February 1 - [[Gyanendra of Nepal|King Gyanendra]] dismisses the government of Sher Bahadur Deuba and assumes direct authority.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.satp.org/satporgtp/countries/nepal/document/papers/05emergencyking.htm|title=Proclamation to the Nation by King Gyanendra|accessdate=11 May 2019}}</ref>
* April 7 - Maoists [[Second Battle of Khara|attack on a Royal Nepal Army base]] in Khara, Rukum fails, leading to death of at least 166 insurgents.
* July 6 - Maoists [[Badarmude bus explosion|bomb a civilian bus]] in Bandarmude, Chitwan killing 38 and injuring around 75.<ref>{{cite web |title=Bandarmude incident a mistake: Dahal|url=https://kathmandupost.ekantipur.com/news/2017-11-26/bandarmude-incident-a-mistake-dahal.html |website=Ekantipur |publisher=Kantipur Publications |accessdate=12 May 2019}}</ref>
* August 30 - September 2 - 11th General Convention of Nepali Congress.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nepalicongress.org/index.php?linkId=2|title=Introduction|website=Nepalicongress.org|accessdate=24 May 2019}}</ref>
