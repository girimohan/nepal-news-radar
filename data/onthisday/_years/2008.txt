{{Year in Nepal|2008}}

The following lists events that happened during '''[[2008]] in [[Nepal]]'''.

==Incumbents==
*[[King of Nepal|Monarch]]: [[Gyanendra of Nepal|Gyanendra]] (until May 28) - monarchy abolished
*[[President of Nepal|President]]: [[Ram Baran Yadav]] (starting 23 July)
*[[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]]: [[Girija Prasad Koirala]] (starting 25 May and ending 18 August), [[Prachanda]] (starting 18 August)
*[[Vice President of Nepal|Vice President]]: [[Parmanand Jha]] (starting 23 July)
*[[Chief Justice of Nepal|Chief Justice]]: [[Kedar Prasad Giri]]

==Events==

===May===
* May 26 - Nepalese authorities ban rallies and mass meetings in [[Kathmandu]] prior to the first meeting of the [[1st Nepalese Constituent Assembly]] which is expected to declare Nepal a republic.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/south_asia/7419772.stm | title=Nepal bans rallies in Kathmandu | date=26 May 2008 | accessdate=25 January 2015}}</ref>
* May 28 - The Nepalese Constituent Assembly meets for the first time after the [[2008 Nepalese Constituent Assembly election]] and votes to abolish the monarchy and establish a republic.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/south_asia/7422900.stm | title=Nepal prepares to become republic | date=28 May 2008 | accessdate=25 January 2015}}</ref>

===June===
[[File:Narayanhiti Palace Museum.jpg|thumb|300px|Narayanhiti Palace]]
* June 11 - The last [[King of Nepal]] [[Gyanendra of Nepal|Gyanendra]] departs from Narayanhiti Palace for the last time after Nepal is declared to be a republic.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.cbc.ca/news/world/ex-king-quits-palace-in-new-republic-of-nepal-1.729285 | title=Ex-king quits palace in new republic of Nepal | date=11 June 2008 | accessdate=25 January 2015}}</ref>

===July===
* July 21 - [[2008 Nepalese presidential election|Nepal's presidential election]] concludes with [[Ram Baran Yadav]] winning with a majority.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.chinadaily.com.cn/world/2008-07/21/content_6863931.htm | title=Nepali presidential re-election voting continues | date=21 July 2008 | accessdate=25 January 2015}}</ref>

===August===
* August 15 - The Nepalese Constituent Assembly elects former Maoist rebel Prachanda as the first Prime Minister of Nepal as a republic.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.abc.net.au/news/2008-08-16/former-maoist-rebel-elected-as-nepalese-pm/478382 | title=Former Maoist rebel elected as Nepalese PM | date=16 August 2008 | accessdate=25 January 2015}}</ref>

==Deaths==
[[Edmund Hillary]] - First person to reach top of Everest.
