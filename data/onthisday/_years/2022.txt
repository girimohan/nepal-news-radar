== Events ==
=== January ===
* 2 January – Seven people are killed and eight injured in a bus accident in [[Palpa District|Palpa]].<ref>{{Cite web|date=2 January 2022|title=Seven killed, eight injured in Palpa bus accident|url=https://thehimalayantimes.com/nepal/seven-killed-eight-injured-in-palpa-bus-accident|url-status=live|access-date=4 January 2022|website=The Himalayan Times|language=en|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220102073651/https://thehimalayantimes.com/nepal/seven-killed-eight-injured-in-palpa-bus-accident |archive-date=2 January 2022 }}</ref>

=== February ===
* 20 February – Hundreds of protestors gather outside the [[Federal Parliament of Nepal|Nepalese parliament]] in [[Kathmandu]] to protest a United States grant from the [[Millennium Challenge Corporation]] for infrastructure projects, stating the deal undermines Nepal's sovereignty. Several protesters are injured following clashes with [[Nepal Police|police]].<ref>{{Cite news |date=2022-02-20 |title=Nepal police fire rubber bullets during US grant protest |language=en-GB |work=BBC News |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-60454475 |access-date=2022-12-30}}</ref>
* 27 February – Federal Parliament of Nepal’s ratifies the $500 million compact between [[Millennium Challenge Corporation's Nepal Compact|MCC]] and the Government of Nepal.<ref>{{Cite web |date=1 March 2022 |title=MCC Statement on Nepal Compact Ratification |url=https://www.mcc.gov/news-and-events/release/stmt-030122-nepal-compact-ratification/ |access-date=29 January 2024 |website=Millennium Challenge Corporation}}</ref>

=== September ===
* 17 September – Twenty-two people are killed and 10 others are injured by a [[landslide]] in [[Achham]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Sharma |first=Gopal |date=2022-09-18 |title=Death toll from western Nepal landslide rises to 22 |language=en |work=Reuters |url=https://www.reuters.com/business/environment/death-toll-western-nepal-landslide-rises-22-2022-09-18/ |access-date=2022-12-30}}</ref>

=== October ===
* 6 October – Sixteen people are killed, and 20 others injured when a bus overturns in [[Bara District]], Nepal, apparently due to speeding.<ref>{{Cite web |title=16 dead in Nepal's traffic accident-Xinhua |url=http://english.news.cn/20221006/0c32184658a34ccb8578942c47058f92/c.html |access-date=2022-10-06 |website=english.news.cn}}</ref> 
* 9 October – President [[Bidya Devi Bhandari]] tests positive for [[COVID-19]] one day after being hospitalized.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nepali president tests positive for COVID-19-Xinhua |url=http://english.news.cn/20221009/68e0cc63fcb347839fa1ebfd791450c0/c.html |access-date=2022-10-10 |website=english.news.cn}}</ref> 
* 12 October – [[2022 South Asian floods]]: At least 33 people are killed during heavy floods, with the [[Karnali Province]] being the most affected.<ref>{{Cite news |date=2022-10-12 |title=At least 33 killed in Nepal flooding and landslides |language=en-GB |work=BBC News |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-63224454 |access-date=2022-10-12}}</ref>

=== November ===
* 9 November – [[2022 Nepal earthquake]]: Six people are killed when a magnitude 5.6 [[List of earthquakes in Nepal|earthquake]] strikes [[Sudurpashchim]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=9 November 2022 |title=Nepal Earthquake Today: 6 killed as 6.3-magnitude earthquake hits Nepal; strong tremors felt in Delhi, neighbouring areas {{!}} World News – Times of India |url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/south-asia/6-3-magnitude-earthquake-hits-nepal-strong-tremors-felt-in-delhi-and-neighbouring-areas/articleshow/95387649.cms |access-date=2022-12-30 |website=The Times of India |language=en}}</ref>
* 20 November – [[2022 Nepalese general election]]: [[Nepalis]] elect the 275 members of the [[House of Representatives (Nepal)|House of Representatives]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-11-20 |title=Voting begins in Nepal amid concerns over economic, political stability |url=https://english.alarabiya.net/News/world/2022/11/20/Voting-begins-in-Nepal-amid-concerns-over-economic-political-stability |access-date=2022-12-30 |website=Al Arabiya English |language=en}}</ref>

=== December ===
* 23 December – French serial killer [[Charles Sobhraj]], who received a life sentence in Nepal for murdering 12 [[Tourism in Nepal|tourists]] between 1963 and 1976, and suspected of murdering also 18 other people, is released from prison [[Compassionate release|due to his old age]] and good behaviour in accordance with a [[Supreme Court of Nepal|Supreme Court]] order. He was deported to France and barred from Nepal for ten years.<ref>{{Cite web |title='Bikini Killer' Charles Sobhraj Freed From Nepal Jail, To Be Deported To France |url=https://www.ndtv.com/world-news/serial-killer-charles-sobhraj-leaves-jail-in-nepal-news-agency-afp-3631799 |access-date=2022-12-30 |website=NDTV.com}}</ref>
