==Events==
===January===
*January 1 - The President calls for winter session of parliament to begin. Only the [[National Assembly (Nepal)|National Assembly]] meets as the [[House of Representatives (Nepal)|House of Representatives]] was dissolved on 20 December 2020.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Dahal|first1=Ashok|date=1 January 2021|title=संसद् अधिवेशन: प्रतिनिधि सभा नभए पनि राष्ट्रिय सभाले गर्न सक्छ यी छ काम|url=https://www.bbc.com/nepali/news-55505119|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210101064520/https://www.bbc.com/nepali/news-55505119 |archive-date=2021-01-01 |access-date=3 January 2021|website=BBC Nepali|language=Nepali}}</ref>
*January 10 - The President ends the winter session of parliament. The [[National Assembly (Nepal)|National Assembly]] was in session for only 10 days.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Bajgain|first1=Raghunath|title=सरकार अल्पमतमा परेपछि राष्ट्रिय सभाको अधिवेशन अन्त्य |url=https://www.onlinekhabar.com/2021/01/922758 |website=Onlinekhabar |access-date=11 January 2021 |language=Nepali}}</ref>
*January 16 - The first winter ascent of [[K2]] by 10 Nepalis.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Arnette |first1=Alan |title=Winter K2 Update: FIRST WINTER K2 SUMMIT!!!! |url=https://www.alanarnette.com/blog/2021/01/16/winter-k2-update-first-winter-k2-summit/ |access-date=16 January 2021}}</ref>

===February===
*February 23 - The [[Supreme Court of Nepal|Supreme Court]] reinstates the dissolved [[House of Representatives (Nepal)|House of Representatives.]]<ref>{{Cite web|title=प्रतिनिधिसभा विघटन बदर गर्ने सर्वोच्चको फैसला, १३ दिनभित्र संसद अधिवेशन बोलाउन आदेश|url=https://nayapatrikadaily.com/news-details/60576/2021-02-23|access-date=2021-02-23|website=Naya Patrika|language=en}}</ref>

===March===
*March 7 - The [[Supreme Court of Nepal|Supreme Court]] annuls the decision of the [[Election Commission, Nepal|Election Commission]] to grant the name [[Nepal Communist Party|Nepal Communist Party (NCP)]] to the party created by the merger of [[Communist Party of Nepal (Unified Marxist–Leninist)]] and [[Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist Centre)]] and positions them to their pre merger status.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Supreme Court awards Nepal Communist Party to Rishiram Kattel|url=https://kathmandupost.com/national/2021/03/07/supreme-court-awards-nepal-communist-party-to-rishiram-kattel|access-date=2021-03-07|website=kathmandupost.com|language=English}}</ref>

=== April ===
* April 14 - Nepali New Year, [[Vikrama Samvat|Bikram Sambat 2078]] begins.
* April 24 - The replica of [[Dharahara|Bhimsen Tower]] is inaugurated. The original tower was destroyed in the [[April 2015 Nepal earthquake]]<ref>{{Cite news|date=2021-04-24|title=Nepal opens replica Dharahara Tower in Kathmandu|language=en-GB|work=BBC News|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-56873438|access-date=2021-05-09}}</ref>

===May===
*May 9 - [[Prithvi Subba Gurung]], Chief Minister of [[Gandaki Province]] resigns ahead of a no confidence vote scheduled for May 10.<ref>{{Cite web|title=गण्डकीका मुख्यमन्त्री पृथ्वीसुब्बा गुरुङले दिए राजीनामा|url=https://www.onlinekhabar.com/2021/05/952405|access-date=2021-05-09|website=Online Khabar|language=en-US}}</ref>
*May 10 - Prime Minister Oli fails to obtain vote of confidence in parliament. He obtains only 93 votes in favor while a faction within his own party boycotts the vote.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Pradhan|first=Tika R.|title=Nepal Prime Minister Oli fails trust vote in Parliament|url=https://kathmandupost.com/politics/2021/05/10/nepal-prime-minister-oli-fails-trust-vote-in-parliament|url-status=live|access-date=2021-05-11|website=kathmandupost.com|language=English|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210510121939/https://kathmandupost.com/politics/2021/05/10/nepal-prime-minister-oli-fails-trust-vote-in-parliament |archive-date=2021-05-10 }}</ref>
*May 13 - KP Sharma Oli is re-appointed prime minister by the President per the provision of Article 76(3) of the constitution. Opposition parties did not claim majority leading to his appointment as the leader of the largest party in parliament.<ref>{{Cite web|title=केपी ओली प्रधानमन्त्री नियुक्त, शपथ शुक्रबार|url=https://www.onlinekhabar.com/2021/05/953625|access-date=2021-05-14|website=Online Khabar|language=en-US}}</ref>

===June===
*June 4 and June 10 - PM KP Sharma Oli expands and reshuffles cabinet resulting with 14 members from CPN(UML) and 11 from Mahantha Thakur faction of PSP-N.
*June 5 - [[Nepali Congress]] joins the government of Karnali Province.
*June 9 - [[Nepali Congress]] joins the government of [[Province No. 2]].
*June 11 - [[Krishna Chandra Nepali Pokharel]] of [[Nepali Congress]] is sworn in as the second chief-minister of [[Gandaki Province|Gandaki]].
*June 22 - Supreme Court issues an interim order annulling the cabinet expansion of June 4 and June 10 by Prime Minister Oli. The order relieves 20 ministers of their positions with the cabinet now composed of only four ministers.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Supreme Court quashes appointment of 20 ministers|url=https://kathmandupost.com/national/2021/06/22/supreme-court-quashes-appointment-of-20-ministers|access-date=2021-06-22|website=kathmandupost.com|language=English}}</ref>

===July===
* July 12 - Supreme Court reinstates the dissolved House of Representatives and issues an order to appoint [[Sher Bahadur Deuba]] as [[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]] per provision of Article 76(5) of the constitution.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Online|first=T. H. T.|date=2021-07-12|title=Supreme Court reinstates House, issues verdict in favour of Deuba's claim|url=https://thehimalayantimes.com/nepal/supreme-court-reinstates-house-issues-verdict-in-favour-of-deubas-claim|access-date=2021-07-14|website=The Himalayan Times|language=en}}</ref>
*July 13 - [[Sher Bahadur Deuba]] from [[Nepali Congress|NC]] takes oath of office as the [[Prime Minister of Nepal|prime minister]] for a fifth term.<ref>{{Cite web|title=देउवा प्रधानमन्त्री नियुक्त, सपथको तयारी|url=https://www.setopati.com/politics/243073/|access-date=2021-07-13|website=Setopati|archive-date=2021-10-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211027220534/https://www.setopati.com/politics/243073/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
*July 27 - [[Prithvi Man Gurung]] and [[Amik Sherchan]] are appointed governor of [[Gandaki Province|Gandaki]] and [[Lumbini Province|Lumbini]] provinces, respectively.

=== August ===
*August 12 - [[Kul Prasad KC]] becomes chief minister of [[Lumbini Province]] after a power sharing agreement between [[Nepali Congress]] and [[Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist Centre)|CPN (Maoist Centre)]]. 
*August 17 - [[Hari Shankar Mishra]] appointed Governor of Province No. 2.
*August 18 -
**[[Astalaxmi Shakya|Ashta Laxmi Shakya]] becomes first women chief minister of any province in Nepal, heading the [[Provincial Assembly of Bagmati Province|Bagmati provincial government.]]
**Two national parties split on same day:
***The largest party in parliament, [[Communist Party of Nepal (Unified Marxist–Leninist)|CPN (UML)]] split to form [[Communist Party of Nepal (Unified Socialist)|CPN (Unified Socialist)]] led by former prime minister [[Madhav Kumar Nepal]].
***[[People's Socialist Party, Nepal (2020)|PSP-N]] split to form [[Loktantrik Samajwadi Party, Nepal|Loktantrik Samajbadi Party Nepal]] led by [[Mahantha Thakur]]. Now, there are 6 national parties in Nepal.

=== November ===
* November 11 - [[2021 Nepal census]] begins.
* November 14 - A massive [[2021 Mount Manapathi avalanche|avalanche]] occurred in Mount Manapathi in Mustang district.
