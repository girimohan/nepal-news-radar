The following events occurred in [[Kingdom of Nepal|Nepal]] in the year [[1951]].

== Incumbents ==

* [[King of Nepal|Monarch]]: [[Gyanendra of Nepal|Gyanendra]] (1950-7 January 1951) and [[Tribhuvan of Nepal|Tribhuvan]] (7 January)
* [[Prime Minister of Nepal|Prime Minister]]: [[Mohan Shumsher Jung Bahadur Rana]] (1948-12 November 1951) and [[Matrika Prasad Koirala]] (16 November)

== Events ==

* November – [[1951 Nepalese revolution]] begins.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|title=Nepal Democracy Day: 7 facts about 1951 revolution|url=https://english.onlinekhabar.com/nepal-democracy-day-facts-about-1951-revolution.html|url-status=live|access-date=11 January 2022|website=OnlineKhabar|language=en-GB|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210219034615/https://english.onlinekhabar.com/nepal-democracy-day-facts-about-1951-revolution.html |archive-date=19 February 2021 }}</ref>
